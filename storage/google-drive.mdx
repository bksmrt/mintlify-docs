Connect a Google Drive account to browse your Drive and import compliance documents into a project's evidence library.

## Before you start

- Your Proof administrator must have configured the Google Drive OAuth client. If not, Google Drive shows as unavailable in the connect list.
- Any member of your institution can connect an account from the **Import from cloud** dialog in a project's evidence tab.

## Permissions Proof requests

When you connect, Google asks you to approve these scopes:

| Scope | Why Proof needs it |
| --- | --- |
| `drive.readonly` | Browse folders and download files. Proof never writes to, moves, or deletes your Drive files. |
| `openid`, `email`, `profile` | Show which Google account the connection belongs to. |

Proof requests offline access, so the connection keeps working after your session ends. Access tokens refresh automatically.

## Connect and import

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the import dialog">
    In a project's evidence tab, click **Import from cloud**, then choose **Google Drive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve access">
    Sign in to Google and approve the requested permissions. Google always shows the consent screen, even if you approved Proof before.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse your Drive">
    Browsing starts at **My Drive**. Open folders to navigate, and use the breadcrumb trail to go back. Items in the trash are hidden.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import a file">
    Click **Import** next to a supported file. The file appears in the evidence tab when processing finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Google-native files

Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides have no downloadable file of their own, so Proof exports them during import:

| Google file type | Imported as |
| --- | --- |
| Google Docs | Plain text (`.txt`) |
| Google Sheets | CSV (`.csv`) |
| Google Slides | Plain text (`.txt`) |

Exported files keep the original name with the new extension added. Formatting, images, and comments are not preserved. For Sheets, only cell values are kept.

<Tip>
  If formatting matters for review, export the document to PDF in Google Drive first, then import the PDF.
</Tip>

## Other supported files

Uploaded files such as PDF, Word (`.docx`), Excel (`.xlsx`, `.xlsm`), CSV, and text files import as they are. See [Which files can be imported](/storage/overview#which-files-can-be-imported).
