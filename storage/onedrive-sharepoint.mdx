Proof connects to Microsoft 365 through Microsoft Graph. OneDrive and SharePoint are separate providers in the connect list because they browse different places:

| Provider | What you browse |
| --- | --- |
| **OneDrive** | The signed-in user's personal OneDrive. |
| **SharePoint** | Document libraries on any SharePoint site the signed-in user can access. |

Connect both if your evidence lives in both places.

## Before you start

- Your Proof administrator must have configured the Microsoft Graph OAuth client. Both providers use the same client, so they are available or unavailable together.
- Your Microsoft 365 tenant may require an administrator to approve Proof before users can consent. If sign-in stops with an approval message, ask your Microsoft 365 administrator to grant consent.

## Permissions Proof requests

| Scope | Why Proof needs it |
| --- | --- |
| `Files.Read.All` | Read files the user can access, including OneDrive and SharePoint libraries. |
| `Sites.Read.All` | List SharePoint sites and their document libraries. |
| `User.Read` | Show which Microsoft account the connection belongs to. |
| `offline_access` | Keep the connection working by refreshing access tokens. |

All scopes are read-only. Proof never changes or deletes your Microsoft 365 files.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the import dialog">
    In a project's evidence tab, click **Import from cloud**, then choose **OneDrive** or **SharePoint**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Microsoft">
    Sign in with your work or school account and approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the connection">
    The connection appears with your account email. Select it to start browsing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Browse OneDrive

Browsing starts at the root of your OneDrive, shown as **Files**. Open folders to navigate, then click **Import** next to a supported file.

## Browse SharePoint

SharePoint browsing has extra levels before you reach files:

1. **Sites**: every SharePoint site your account can access.
2. **Libraries**: the document libraries on the selected site, such as **Documents**.
3. **Folders and files**: the contents of the selected library.

<Tip>
  If a site is missing from the list, confirm your Microsoft account has at least read access to it in SharePoint.
</Tip>

## Supported files

PDF, Word (`.docx`), Excel (`.xlsx`, `.xlsm`), CSV, and text files can be imported. Legacy Office formats (`.doc`, `.xls`) and PowerPoint files appear in the browser but are marked **Unsupported**. Save them in a supported format first. See [Which files can be imported](/storage/overview#which-files-can-be-imported).
