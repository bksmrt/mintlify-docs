The **Documents** page is your institution's working register of policies, handbooks, templates, and other compliance records. Compliance officers, accreditation liaisons, and institutional admins use it to draft, revise, approve, and archive the documents that support accreditation projects and regulatory compliance.

## How to get there

Click **Documents** in the main sidebar.

## What you see

The page opens as a journal-styled register with the following sections.

### Search and filters

- A search bar labeled **Consult the register…** filters records by title.
- **Subject filters** show the most common tags used across your institution's documents. Click a tag to filter the list. The active filters appear as chips.
- A **Standing** facet lets you narrow the list by status.

### Status tabs

The register is organized by standing. Each tab shows a count:

- **All**: every record in the register.
- **Drafts**: records still being written or revised.
- **In Review**: records submitted for internal review.
- **Approved**: records that have passed review but are not yet published.
- **Published**: active, institution-facing records.
- **Archived**: inactive records kept for reference.

### Register list

Each row shows:

- A row number and the record title.
- **Subjects** (tags) assigned to the record.
- A provenance line showing whether the document is cited as evidence in reviews and how many other records it is linked to.
- **Standing** label with a color tone.
- **Filed** date, **Edited** date, and edition number.
- A **Pin** action to bookmark the record to the top of your personal view.

### Pagination

The register shows 25 records per page. Use the pager at the bottom to move between pages.

### Review Calendar context rail (compliance roles only)

If your role is **Institutional Admin** or **Compliance Officer**, a collapsible **Review Calendar** rail appears on the right. It surfaces upcoming policy reviews, rule deadlines, and disclosure dates for quick context while you work in Documents.

## What you can do here

- **Create a new record**: click **Enter a Record** to add a new document directly in the register.
- **View a record**: open the document detail page to read content, see compliance mappings, and review history.
- **Edit a record**: if your role allows, open a record in edit mode to change its title, standing, content, subjects, or owning department.
- **Edit tags inline**: quickly add or remove tags on a record without opening the detail page.
- **Pin records**: bookmark frequently used records so they always appear at the top of the register.
- **Download a record**: save the record's content as a plain text file.
- **Share a record**: copy a deep link to the record onto your clipboard.
- **Archive or restore**: move a record to **Archived** to remove it from active lists, or restore an archived record back to **Drafts**.
- **Delete a record**: permanently remove a record. The confirmation dialog offers **Archive instead** as a safer, reversible choice.
- **Select multiple records**: turn on selection mode to choose several records at once, then export them as a CSV, archive them in bulk, or delete them together.
- **Import from cloud storage**: cloud import is not on this page. Use **Import from cloud** on a project's **Evidence** tab instead. See [Add evidence and match it to standards](/user-guide/accreditation/how-to/add-and-match-evidence).
