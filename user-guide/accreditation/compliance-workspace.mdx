---
title: "Compliance workspace"
description: "Work through each accreditation standard in a project: write the narrative, resolve issues, and attach evidence."
---

The compliance workspace is where your team does the day-to-day work of an accreditation project. It lists the standards and compliance gaps for the project and opens each standard in an editor where you write the narrative, review flagged issues, and link evidence.

To get there, open a project from [Projects](/user-guide/accreditation/projects), then choose **Compliance workspace** from the project's **Actions** menu.

## What you see

### Header

The header shows the project name and these controls:

- **Self-Study Document**: opens the full [self-study document](/user-guide/accreditation/self-study) for the project.
- **Actions** menu:
  - **Packet**: opens the self-study packet for the project.
  - **Activity**: shows recent activity on the project.
  - **Scan evidence**: scans your documents for evidence across every standard. Appears when the project has compliance gaps.
- Collaborator avatars show who else is working in the workspace right now. **Only you** means nobody else is active.

### Standard editor

Select a standard to open it. The editor contains:

| Area | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Narrative | Your written response to the standard, organized by section |
| **Issues** | Findings on the narrative, such as **Unsupported claim**, **Vague claim**, **Hedging language**, **Passive voice**, and **Evidence needed** |
| **Evidence** | Evidence slots for the standard and suggested document matches |
| **Narrative Score** | How well the narrative is supported, from weak to **Strong** |
| **Review Feedback** | Comments from reviewers |
| **Source Compliance Gap** | The gap that this standard's work responds to |

When a section is finished, select **Submit for Review** under it to send it to the approver. If the approver requests changes, edit the section and submit it again.

### Assistant

The assistant walks you through a standard one requirement at a time. For each requirement, it asks what the program actually does and which record shows it. Proof then writes the narrative sentence from your answers and that record together.

To open it, click the assistant button (**Assistant ·** followed by the standard's number) in the workspace header. The menu shows where the standard stands and these options:

- **Work through** followed by the standard's number: starts the walk. The first time, the walk opens as a full page. After that, it opens in a panel beside the editor.
- **What should I do first?**: opens the requirement that needs you most, and says why.
- **Pick up where I left off**: returns to the requirement you were on.
- **Ask anything**: opens the panel in **Chat**.

The panel takes the place of the **Issues** rail. Click **Issues** at the foot of the panel to switch back. Drag the panel's left edge to resize it.

Switch between two ways of working at the top of the panel. Both work on the same walk, so switching loses nothing:

- **Steps**: answer each requirement's questions in fields, then link or upload the record that shows it. When the record shows the sentence, click **place it** to put the sentence in the narrative section. If no record shows it yet, click **place anyway…** and give a reason. The sentence goes in marked open, and Proof keeps your reason on its history. Click **Skip for now** to move on without finishing a requirement.
- **Chat**: type your answers, or ask about the standard, its rules, or your records. Press **Ctrl+Enter** (**⌘+Enter** on a Mac) to send. When you ask the assistant to find a policy or document, it lists the records it found with the passage that matched. Click **Link** to use one as the requirement's evidence. Records found in Chat also appear in **Steps** under **Found in Chat**.

The assistant only searches and links records your role lets you read. Your Chat thread on each standard is kept, so you can return to it later. When the walk is done, submit each section for review in the editor as usual.

### Empty workspace

If you see **No compliance gaps detected**, Proof has not yet found gaps for this project. Gaps are identified when a disclosure document is imported and parsed. Select **Create Gaps from Disclosure** to try again.

## What you can do here

- Write and revise the narrative for a standard. Rewrite options include **Elaborate**, **Shorten**, and **Academic**, and you can **Undo rewrite**. See [Work a standard and close gaps](/user-guide/accreditation/how-to/work-a-standard).
- Walk a standard requirement by requirement with the [assistant](#assistant).
- Resolve or dismiss issues from the **Issues** rail. See [Work a standard and close gaps](/user-guide/accreditation/how-to/work-a-standard).
- Use **Find evidence** and **Confirm** a match to link a document. See [Add evidence and match it to standards](/user-guide/accreditation/how-to/add-and-match-evidence).
- Open the full self-study when the standards are ready. See [Prepare the self-study](/user-guide/accreditation/how-to/prepare-self-study).
