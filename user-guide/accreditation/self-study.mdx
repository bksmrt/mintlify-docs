The **Self-study document** gathers every narrative section your team has written into one cohesive, ordered document. Compliance officers and accreditation liaisons use this screen to review the full narrative, reorder sections, insert supporting tables and charts, and export a PDF for committee or accreditor review.

## How to get here

Open a project from [Projects](/user-guide/accreditation/projects), open its [Compliance workspace](/user-guide/accreditation/compliance-workspace), and select **Self-Study Document**.

## What you see

### Document header

- **Project name** and a narrative version badge (for example, v3).
- **Pin version**: lock the self-study to the accreditor catalog version as of now.
- **Save version**: capture the current state so you can compare or restore it later.
- **History**: open a list of saved versions with a count badge.
- **Compare**: open a side-by-side diff between any two saved versions, or between a saved version and the current document.
- **Export PDF**: download the assembled document.

### Narrative sections

Each section appears as a card in the order it will export. Every card shows:

- A **drag handle** so you can reorder the section.
- Its **position number** in the document.
- The **section title**.
- A **type badge**: Claim, Demonstration, Full Narrative, or General.
- The linked **standard number** and **version**.
- A **review-note icon** with a count when comments exist.
- Up and down arrow buttons to move the section.
- A **Duplicate** button.

### Section editor

Each card contains a rich text editor pre-filled with the section content. Changes are tracked locally until you save.

- **Save**: store the updated text.
- **Revert**: discard local changes and restore the last saved text.

### Embedded elements

Below the editor, any previously inserted elements render inline:

- **Data tables** with column headers and rows.
- **Figures and charts**: bar, line, or pie charts with data labels.
- **Cross-references**: links to other sections, figures, or appendices with an optional note.

Each element has a **delete** button that appears on hover.

### Insert menu

Every section card has an **Insert** dropdown with three choices:

- **Data table**: enter comma-separated columns and comma-separated rows.
- **Figure / chart**: choose bar, line, or pie, then enter label-and-value pairs.
- **Cross-reference**: link to another section, a figure, or an appendix and add an optional note.

### Review notes

Click the **review-note icon** on any section to open the project comment thread and add a note tied to that section. Hovering the icon shows a preview of the latest note. If notes exist, a **View note** (or **View all _N_ notes**) link jumps directly to the comment thread.

### Version pinning

Click **Pin version** to open the **Catalog version pinning** dialog.

- If the document is not pinned, it tracks the latest accreditor catalog automatically.
- Click **Pin** to freeze the catalog version. Proof records the date and time.
- Once pinned, the dialog shows a **delta summary** of every standard added, removed, or changed since the pin date. Each changed standard lists affected narrative sections and evidence slots you can open directly.
- Click **Unpin** to resume tracking the latest catalog.

### Version history

Click **History** to open the **Document versions** dialog. Each saved version shows:

- A **label** you entered when saving.
- A **trigger badge**: Manual for user-saved versions, or Auto for versions captured after edits, reorders, duplicates, element changes, or restores.
- A **section count**.
- The **creator name** and **time since save**.
- **Compare to current**: open the diff view against the live document.
- **Restore**: roll the document back to that version. Proof auto-saves a pre-restore version first.

### Compare versions

Click **Compare** to open the **Compare document versions** dialog.

- Choose a **From version** and a **To version** (or **Current document**).
- Proof shows a side-by-side diff of every section, highlighting additions, removals, and changes.

## What you can do here

- **Reorder sections** by dragging cards or using the up and down arrow buttons.
- **Edit narrative text** in any section and save your changes.
- **Duplicate a section** to reuse its structure elsewhere.
- **Insert tables, charts, or cross-references** to strengthen the narrative.
- **Add review notes** on individual sections for team feedback.
- **Pin the catalog version** to freeze requirements and track future changes.
- **Save named versions** before major reviews or committee meetings.
- **Compare versions** to see exactly what changed between drafts.
- **Restore an older version** if the committee wants to revert.
- **Export a PDF** for external distribution.
