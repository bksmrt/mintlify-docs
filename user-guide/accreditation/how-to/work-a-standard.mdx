Each standard in an accreditation project needs a well-supported narrative. This guide walks through taking one standard from open gap to done.

## Before you start

- You need a project with compliance gaps. Gaps are identified when a disclosure document is imported and parsed.
- You should be on the project team or committee.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the compliance workspace">
    Open your project from [Projects](/user-guide/accreditation/projects), then choose **Compliance workspace** from the **Actions** menu. See [Compliance workspace](/user-guide/accreditation/compliance-workspace).

    If you see **No compliance gaps detected**, select **Create Gaps from Disclosure**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a standard and claim a section">
    Select a standard. Review the **Source Compliance Gap** to understand what is missing. Use the section menu to **Mark in progress** so the team knows you own it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write or revise the narrative">
    Draft the response in the narrative editor. To adjust tone or length, use **Elaborate**, **Shorten**, or **Academic**. If a rewrite misses, select **Undo rewrite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through the Issues rail">
    Open **Issues** and address each finding, such as **Unsupported claim**, **Vague claim**, **Hedging language**, or **Passive voice**. **Resolve** what you fix and **Dismiss** what does not apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link evidence">
    For findings marked **Evidence needed**, use **Find evidence** and **Confirm** a document. See [Add evidence and match it to standards](/user-guide/accreditation/how-to/add-and-match-evidence).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the score and finish">
    Check the **Narrative Score** and any **Review Feedback**. When the section is complete, select **Mark done**. If you are stuck, select **Mark blocked** so a lead can help.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The section's status updates for everyone in the workspace, and project progress reflects it.
- Finished narratives flow into the [self-study document](/user-guide/accreditation/self-study).

<Tip>
  Check the collaborator avatars in the workspace header before editing, so you do not overwrite a colleague's work.
</Tip>
