Each standard in an accreditation project needs a well-supported narrative. This guide walks through taking one standard from open gap to done.

## Before you start

- You need a project that answers to an agency whose standards have reached your college. Gaps come from the agency's letter or disclosure, or you raise them yourself.
- You should be on the project team or committee.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the compliance workspace">
    Open your project from [Projects](/user-guide/accreditation/projects), then choose **Compliance workspace** from the **Actions** menu. See [Compliance workspace](/user-guide/accreditation/compliance-workspace).

    If the project answers to no agency yet, choose one and click **Set agency**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a standard and see where it stands">
    Select a standard. Switch **Read as** to **Standard** to see each requirement with its standing and the sentence that answers it. Review the **Source Compliance Gap** to understand what is missing.

    New to the room? Click the **p.** mark in the header to open the assistant. On **Needs you**, click **Tour the Workspace**, or click **Start** beside **Work through** to answer the standard one requirement at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write or revise the narrative">
    Switch **Read as** back to **Narrative**. Rest on a passage and click **Edit** to change its words in place, then **Save**. To adjust tone or length, use **Elaborate**, **Shorten**, or **Academic**. If a rewrite misses, select **Undo rewrite**. If you can't edit a section, click **Suggest** to propose a change to its writers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through the Issues rail">
    Open **Issues** and address each finding, such as **Unsupported claim**, **Vague claim**, **Hedging language**, or **Passive voice**. **Resolve** what you fix and **Dismiss** what does not apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link evidence">
    For findings marked **Evidence needed**, use **Find evidence** and **Confirm** a document. See [Add evidence and match it to standards](/user-guide/accreditation/how-to/add-and-match-evidence).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluate the gap">
    On the gap's card, click **Evaluate** and write its cause and effect, and rate its likelihood and impact. See [Gaps and findings](/user-guide/accreditation/gaps).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check progress and finish">
    Check the progress bar beside the section and any **Review Feedback**. A section whose work is done shows one unbroken sage bar. When the section is complete, click **Submit for review** on its review line to send it to the approver.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The approver approves the section, requests changes, or declines it. If they request changes, edit the section and submit it again.
- When the gap is ready to close, the person who agreed its cause is asked **Is the cause resolved?** The gap closes on a **Yes**. See [Gaps and findings](/user-guide/accreditation/gaps#the-cause-question).
- Finished narratives flow into the [self-study document](/user-guide/accreditation/self-study).

<Tip>
  On a project shared with a consulting firm, check the collaborator avatars in the workspace header before editing, so you do not overwrite a colleague's work.
</Tip>
