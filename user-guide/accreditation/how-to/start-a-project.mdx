This guide shows you how to create a new project in Proof. Projects let you organize accreditation and compliance work, assign team members, and track progress through phases.

## Before you start

You need one of the following to start a project:

- A regulatory change you want to respond to. You can start a project directly from the **Regulatory Intelligence** screen.
- A clear initiative name and type if you are creating a project from scratch on the **Projects** screen.

## Start a project from a regulatory change

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the regulatory change">
    Go to **Regulatory Intelligence** and open the change you want to address. This is the fastest way to start a project because Proof pre-fills the project details for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the project creation flow">
    Click **Create Project**. Proof opens the **Create Project from Regulatory Change** screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the urgency summary">
    At the top of the screen, Proof shows three summary cards:

    - **Time to Effective**: the number of days until the change takes effect
    - **Recommended Approach**: the timeline approach Proof suggests based on urgency
    - **Potential Stakeholders**: how many team members Proof has identified

    These cards help you decide how aggressively to schedule the work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a timeline approach">
    In the **Timeline Approach** section, Proof offers up to three options:

    - **Conservative**: longer timeline, lower risk
    - **Moderate**: balanced timeline and risk
    - **Aggressive**: shorter timeline, higher risk

    The recommended option is marked with a **Recommended** badge. Click the card you prefer. A checkmark appears on the selected card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm project details">
    In the **Project Details** card, review the pre-filled fields:

    - **Initiative Name**
    - **Initiative Type**
    - **Department** (optional)
    - **Linked Entity** (pre-set to the regulatory change)
    - **Description**
    - **Primary Objective**

    Edit any field as needed. If the initiative name already exists, Proof warns you and blocks creation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a governance model">
    On the final review screen, choose a **Governance model** from the dropdown:

    - **Consensus**: every required reviewer must weigh in
    - **Parliamentary**: quorum and majority of votes cast decide
    - **Consultative**: input is advisory; the lead records the outcome

    Proof suggests a default based on your institution's ethos, but you can change it before the first committee vote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the project">
    Click **Create Project**. Proof creates the project, initializes its phases, and opens a confirmation dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the team (optional)">
    In the confirmation dialog, click **Set Up Team** to assign roles and generate tasks automatically. Click **Skip for now** if you want to add team members later from the project detail screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start a project from scratch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Projects screen">
    Click **Projects** in the sidebar. If this is your first project, click **Enter the First Project** in the empty state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the creation dialog">
    Click the **Enter a Project** button at the top of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the project details">
    Complete the form:

    - **Initiative Name**: a unique name for the project
    - **Initiative Type**: choose from Compliance, Curriculum, Faculty, Scheduling, or General
    - **Department**: required for Curriculum and Faculty projects; optional for others
    - **Link to Entity**: optionally link to a compliance rule, policy, regulatory change, or audit cycle
    - **Description**: scope and goals
    - **Primary Objective**: helps Proof generate accurate timeline recommendations
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze feasibility">
    Click **Analyze Feasibility**. Proof generates timeline variants for you to compare.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a timeline variant">
    Review the three timeline options. Each card shows:

    - timeline in days
    - risk level
    - estimated effort
    - key milestones
    - resource requirements
    - considerations

    Click the card you want, then click **Continue with Selection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and create">
    Review the project summary, confirm or change the **Governance model**, then click **Create Project**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- If you chose **Set Up Team**, Proof takes you to the project detail screen with the team setup wizard open. You can assign members to roles and Proof generates tasks based on the project type and timeline.
- If you skipped team setup, the project appears in your **Projects** list with all phases initialized. You can open it at any time to add team members, upload evidence, and work through standards.
- The project is automatically linked to the originating regulatory change or entity, so you can trace the work back to its source.

<Tip>
  Projects started from a regulatory change skip the feasibility analysis step because Proof already knows the urgency and recommends a timeline. This saves time when you need to respond quickly.
</Tip>
