title: "Prepare the self-study"
description: "Assemble the narrative sections into the self-study, review it with your team, and export a PDF for your accreditor."
---

The self-study is the report you submit to your accreditor. This guide covers turning finished standards into a reviewed, versioned, exported document.

## Before you start

- Most standards should be marked done. See [Work a standard and close gaps](/user-guide/accreditation/how-to/work-a-standard).
- Evidence should be linked. See [Add evidence and match it to standards](/user-guide/accreditation/how-to/add-and-match-evidence).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the self-study">
    From the [Compliance workspace](/user-guide/accreditation/compliance-workspace), select **Self-Study Document**. This opens the [Self-study document](/user-guide/accreditation/self-study).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin the catalog version">
    Select **Pin version** and then **Pin** so the requirements stay fixed while you write. Proof shows any standards that change after the pin date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order the sections">
    Drag sections into the order your accreditor expects, or use **Move up** and **Move down**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Polish each section">
    Edit text, then **Save**. Use **Insert** to add a **Data table**, **Figure / chart**, or **Cross-reference**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect team feedback">
    Use the review-note icon on a section to leave notes for reviewers, and read their replies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a version before review">
    Select **Save version** and give it a label, such as "Committee draft". Use **Compare** to see what changed since the last version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Select **Export PDF** to download the assembled document.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- Saved versions appear in **History**, where you can **Compare to current** or **Restore** an older version. Proof saves the current state before any restore.
- From the compliance workspace, the **Packet** action opens the self-study packet for the project.
