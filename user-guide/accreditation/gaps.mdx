A gap is work a standard still needs: missing evidence, a policy that does not cover a requirement, or a status that falls short. Every gap belongs to a project. Its card in the [Compliance workspace](/user-guide/accreditation/compliance-workspace) and its row on the project's **Related** tab carry what Proof knows about it, from the moment it is raised to the moment it closes.

## Raise a gap

Gaps come from an accreditor's letter or disclosure, or you raise them yourself:

- In the compliance workspace, switch **Read as** to **Standard**. A requirement that nothing shows offers **Raise as a gap**. Click it to open a gap on the project. The row then reads **gap raised in this project**.
- In Certification Studio, **Raise as a gap** on a requirement part no policy shows opens a small box. Choose the project under **In** and click **Raise**. If the agency has no open project, the box says so, because a gap must belong to a project.

A gap you raise starts **Not yet rated** and offers **Evaluate**, like every other gap.

## Evaluate a gap

Each gap card has an **Evaluation** block with three lines:

| Line | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Cause** | Why the gap exists, and who you **Agreed with** on it |
| **Effect** | What the gap puts at risk |
| **Rating** | **Likelihood** (**Unlikely**, **Possible**, **Likely**) × **Impact** (**Minor**, **Moderate**, **Major**), which sets the severity: **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, or **Critical** |

Click **Evaluate** (or **Edit** once a gap has an evaluation), fill in the lines, and save. Until a gap is rated, the line reads **Not yet rated** with the severity it was given.

<Tip>
If **Handwritten first pass** is on in your profile, a gap's first evaluation opens as a page of paper. The assistant asks one question at a time, and you answer in your own words. See [The handwritten first pass](/user-guide/accreditation/compliance-workspace#the-handwritten-first-pass).
</Tip>

## The cause question

When a gap with a written cause is ready to close, Proof does not close it straight away. It asks the person who agreed the cause (or, if no one did, the person who wrote it): **Is the cause resolved?**

The question appears at the foot of the gap's card, on the [Approvals](/user-guide/approvals/approvals) page under **Compliance** › **Cause**, and in your [Inbox](/user-guide/approvals/inbox) **Approvals** view.

- **Yes**: confirm, and the gap closes. Your answer goes on the gap's and the project's **History**.
- **No**: write what remains, then click **Send No**. The gap stays open, and your words become a new evidence ask on it, **Shows the cause is resolved**. Proof asks the question again once that ask is shown.

An answer is final once given. The card keeps the record: the answer, who gave it, when, and any earlier answers.

### Another look

If a finding comes back under a gap that was already resolved, Proof asks the same person again, under the label **Another look**: **Is the cause still resolved?**

- **Yes** keeps the gap resolved and dismisses the finding that came back.
- **No**, with your words, reopens the gap with the finding open on it.

If no one answers by the date shown, Proof reopens the gap.

## Repeat findings

When a new gap opens on a standard where an earlier gap at the college was resolved, the new gap wears a **Repeat** stamp. The line beside it says which standard, whether the agency cited it or the college found it, and the month it was resolved, for example *of 10.7 · found by the college · resolved Mar 2026*. Click **Open it** to see the earlier gap in its own project.

The stamp shows on the gap's card, its row on the project's **Related** tab, and the phase story report.

## When a corrective action runs late

A gap's corrective action is a task with a due date. If the task runs past its due date by more than the grace your college allows, Proof asks the compliance officers and institutional admins: **Allow the delay?** The grace depends on the gap's rating. Your institutional admin sets it in Ethos configuration: 30 days for a high gap by default, half that for a critical gap, twice it for medium, and three times for low.

The **Delay** card on Approvals and in the Inbox shows the corrective action, the assignee's latest reason, and any earlier allowance. To decide:

1. Choose a date in **Allow the delay until**.
2. Click **Approve** to allow the delay until that date, or **Request changes** to require action. Requiring action sends your reason to the assignee on the task's log.

An allowed delay shows on the gap as **Delay allowed until** followed by the date. If the gap is still open the day after that date, Proof asks again.

### Risk escalation

Your college can name one role (**Dean**, **Provost**, **President**, or **Board**) under **Risk escalation** on the Institution Profile. Only an institutional admin can change it, in the profile's edit state. When a role is named:

- A delay no one decides within half its grace is handed up to whoever holds that role. The compliance officers and admins can still decide it.
- A second allowance on the same gap, or any allowance on a critical gap, waits for that role to confirm before the gap wears it.
