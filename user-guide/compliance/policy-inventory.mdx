The Policy Inventory is the central register for every institutional policy your college or university maintains. Compliance officers, accreditation liaisons, and institutional admins use it to track policy status, review currency, rule coverage, and attached evidence.

## How to get here

Click **Policy Inventory** in the sidebar.

## What you see

### Register list

The inventory opens as a journal register. Each row is one policy and shows:

- **Number**: position in the register
- **Policy, Summary and Subjects**: the policy title, a short summary, and its category or tags
- **Standing**: Draft, Review, Approved, Published, or Archived
- **Last edited**: the most recent edit date

Pinned policies appear at the top of the list and are marked with a subtle stripe.

### Subject filter

A row of category chips sits above the register. Click a chip to filter to that category; click it again to clear the filter.

### Standing facets

A filter bar lets you scope the list by status:

- **All**
- **Drafts**
- **In Review**
- **Approved**
- **Published**
- **Archived**

Each facet shows a count.

### Search

Use the search field to find policies by title or policy code.

### Review Calendar pane (desktop)

On wide screens, a sticky side pane shows upcoming policy review deadlines. It mirrors the Review Calendar and keeps deadlines in view while you browse the inventory.

## What you can do here

- **Open a policy**: click any row title to open the policy detail page
- **Create a policy**: click **New Policy** to open a dialog. Enter a policy code, category, title, summary, and content, then click **Create Policy**
- **Import policies**: click **Import** to open the AI-powered document import dialog
- **Edit a policy**: from the row menu, choose **Edit** (available to institutional admins, compliance officers, department heads, and anyone an admin has granted policy editing by name)
- **View history**: from the row menu, choose **View History** to see every change and edition
- **Pin or unpin**: from the row menu, choose **Pin Policy** or **Unpin Policy** to keep key policies at the top of the list
- **Link documents**: from the row menu, choose **Link Documents** to attach evidence. A dialog opens with a searchable document list, suggested documents, and a scope selector (Primary, Supporting, or Project Only)
- **View in Certification Studio**: from the row menu, open the policy in Certification Studio
- **Archive**: from the row menu, choose **Archive** to move a policy out of the active inventory while keeping its links intact
- **Unarchive**: on the Archived facet, choose **Unarchive** to restore a policy to active coverage
- **Remove**: from the row menu, choose **Remove** to retire a policy from coverage. Its versions, pins, and evidence are kept, but its rule links are marked removed

### Hide demo data

A toggle lets you hide seeded demo policies so you only see real institutional records.

### Statements read

Click **Statements read** to open the policy readings queue. It lists every policy whose statement was read against the institution's compliance requirements and shows where confirmations or flags are needed.

## Policy detail page

When you open a policy, you see a journal record with these tabs:

- **Content**: the full policy text and summary
- **Related**: a standards review panel and a Where Used panel
- **Compliance**: opens the anchored reading page for confirmations and flags
- **History**: every change and edition, with comparison between versions

The byline above the title shows the policy code, effective date, last reviewed date, next review due date, source document (if imported), status, and archive state.

### Standards review (Related tab)

If the policy is linked to compliance standards, each standard appears as a row with:

- The rule title
- Whether the standard has changed since the last review
- The due date and last reviewed date
- **Confirm** and **Flag** buttons

Confirming restarts the standard's review clock. Flagging opens a compliance gap and prompts you to describe what fails. After selecting decisions, click **Enter review** to submit.

If the policy has no linked standards, the default annual cadence applies. You can still mark the policy reviewed with the **Mark policy reviewed** button.

### Review Standing rail (desktop)

On wide screens, a side rail shows:

- Standards that need review
- Standards that are current
- Periodic review deadlines
- Any linked standard sunset dates

A currency meter shows how many of your linked standards are current versus total.
