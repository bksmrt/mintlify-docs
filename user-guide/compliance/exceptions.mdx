The **Exceptions** page is the register of every departure a department has asked for from one of the college's compliance rules: what was asked, who decides it, and until when it holds. Department members ask for exceptions here, and compliance officers and institutional admins decide them.

## How to get here

Click **Exceptions** in the sidebar, under **Management**. Institutional admins and compliance officers see it in the sidebar.

You can also open it from a rule's row menu in the [Compliance Registry](/user-guide/compliance/compliance-registry): choose **Request exception** on a rule that allows exceptions.

## What you see

### Register list

Each row is one exception and shows:

- **Rule code**: in the number column
- **Exception, reason and terms**: the rule's title and the department, the justification, the change asked for (for example **−5**, or **Does not apply to the college** for a federal statute), who asked and when, and any conditions
- **Status**: **Pending**, **Approved**, **Declined**, or **Expired**
- **Holds until**: the exception's expiration date

Pending exceptions come first, then the rest by the day they hold until, soonest first.

### Search and filters

- Use the search field to find exceptions by rule, department, the person who asked, or the reason.
- Click the filter icon at the right end of the search field to open **Views & filters**, and choose one or more **Standing** options to scope the list. Each option shows a count. Save the search and filters as a view under **Saved views** to come back to them.

### Who reads what

- Institutional admins, compliance officers, and others who read the college's History see every exception.
- A department head sees the exceptions of her own departments.
- Anyone else sees the exceptions they filed themselves.

## Request an exception

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    Click **Request** in the toolbar. The **Request Exception** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the department">
    An exception is filed for the department you belong to, and the dialog names it. Compliance officers and institutional admins choose any department. If you belong to no department, the compliance office files exceptions for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the rule">
    Under **Compliance Rule**, pick the rule. Only rules that are in force and allow exceptions are listed, along with the federal statutes that apply to your college.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the change">
    Use the **Delta Value** slider to set how far the department departs from the rule. The slider stays inside the range the rule allows.

    For a federal statute, there is no slider. The dialog says what the exception means: **Does not apply to the college**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explain and set terms">
    Enter a **Justification**. Add any **Conditions** and an **Expiration Date** if they apply. A federal statute's exception needs an **Expiration Date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Request**. The exception appears as **Pending** and goes to the people who decide it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decide an exception

Compliance officers and institutional admins decide exceptions. The same decision card appears here, on the **Approvals** page, and in the Inbox's **Approvals** tab.

1. On a pending row, choose **Decide** from the row menu, or click the row's title.
2. Read what the exception does: the rule, the range it allows, the change for the department, the reason, and its terms.
3. Click **Approve** or **Decline** and give your reason. The person who asked is told, with your reason.

An approved exception marks the department's compliance item for that rule **Exception granted**, holding the department to the adjusted threshold until the exception expires. The day after it expires, the exception reads **Expired** and the item goes back to review.

## When a federal statute does not apply

Your college's federal statutes follow its Institutional Profile. If one of them does not apply to your college, ask for an exception to it here as you would for a rule. The compliance office decides it on **Approvals**.

While an approved statute exception stands, the statute is off the [Compliance calendar](/user-guide/compliance/compliance-calendar) and the score for the college. On the **Federal statutes** tab of [Manage Compliance](/user-guide/compliance/manage-compliance#federal-statutes-tab), the statute reads **Exception granted**, with who asked, who approved it, and until when. It applies again the day after the exception ends.

## Other actions

- **History**: from the row menu, open every change to the exception.

<Tip>
For an exportable view of every exception in force, and those granted or expired in the last 90 days, open the **Exceptions register** report in Report Studio. It lists each exception's rule, department, change, decider, expiry, and standing, with CSV, Excel, and PDF exports.
</Tip>
