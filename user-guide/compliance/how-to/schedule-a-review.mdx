You can schedule a review for any active policy and then record the outcome against its linked compliance standards. Completing a review updates the policy's standing and removes the overdue notice from the **Review Calendar**.

## Before you start

- You need the **Compliance Officer**, **Institutional Admin**, or **Department Head** role.
- The policy must be in **Active** or **Published** status. Draft or archived policies do not show review controls.
- The policy should have at least one linked standard so the review is standard driven. A policy with no linked standards can still be reviewed on a periodic schedule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the policy record">
    Go to **Policy Inventory** and click the policy you want to review. The policy opens on the **Content** tab of the detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the review due notice">
    If the review date has passed or a linked standard has changed, a **Review is due** notice appears near the top of the page. The notice shows the next review deadline and whether any standard has drifted. Click **Review against standards** in the notice, or open the **Related** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review each standard">
    In the **Related** tab, the **Review Currency, by Standard** section lists every linked standard. Each row shows:

    - The standard title
    - Whether the standard has changed and why
    - The due date and how long since the last review

    For each standard, choose one action:

    - **Confirm**: the policy still satisfies the standard. This restarts the standard's review clock.
    - **Flag**: the policy no longer satisfies the standard. This opens a compliance gap and prompts you to describe what fails. Type the reason in the field that appears below the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the review">
    After you have chosen **Confirm** or **Flag** for at least one standard, the summary at the bottom of the panel shows how many you confirmed and how many you flagged. Click **Enter review** to save the decisions. If the policy has no linked standards, click **Mark policy reviewed** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the result">
    A toast message appears confirming how many standards were confirmed, flagged, and whether any gaps were opened. The **Review Calendar** refreshes automatically, and the policy's next review date updates based on the earliest linked standard deadline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The policy's **Review due** date advances to the next cycle.
- Confirmed standards show a new **Current through** date.
- Flagged standards create open findings that appear in the **Inbox** and on the **Review Calendar** under **Rule deadlines**.
- The **History** tab of the policy records the review entry with a timestamp.

<Tip>
  You can also reach the **Review Calendar** from the sidebar or from the mini calendar on the **Policy Inventory** page. Click any dot on a date to see what is due that day.
</Tip>
