You collect policy readings to see how each policy statement aligns with the requirements your institution is held against. The reading surface shows the policy text alongside each requirement pairing, so you can confirm, correct, or dismiss the engine's findings.

## Before you start

- The policy text must be imported so the engine can read it.
- You need the **Policy Inventory** role or equivalent access.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the proposal queue">
    Go to **Policy Inventory**. Click the **Statements read** button on any policy row that shows a reading count. This opens the **Statements read** list, which is the entry point into the reading workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the list">
    The queue shows one row per policy. Each row displays the policy name, its standing, the stated versus required figures, and who reviewed it. The header tally tells you how many statements state a limit and how many need attention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a policy reading">
    Click a policy row. The reading workspace opens with three panes: a left navigation pane, the policy document in the center, and a right work panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the marked sentence">
    In the center pane, the policy text is shown with marked sentences. Each marked sentence has a dot in the margin and a citation button. Click the sentence or the citation to open the requirement pairing in the right panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the requirement pairing">
    In the right panel, each requirement appears as a row. Click a row to open it in place. The open row shows:

    - The standard header, which links to the standard's source.
    - The requirement's own sentence.
    - Your stated figure versus the required figure.
    - The verdict sentence that states the finding.
    - A **Next step** link when the move is on another record.
    - An **Analysis** disclosure with supporting data and provenance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the source">
    Click the standard header inside an open row to open the source view. This shows the standard number, name, and requirement text, with a link to the full standard page. Click **Back** to return to the row list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress">
    Use the state counts above the row list to see how many readings need attention, are not yet agreed, are waiting elsewhere, or are resolved. Click any count to open the first requirement in that state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- Confirmed readings are recorded as resolved.
- Readings that need attention stay open until you or another reviewer acts.
- Dismissed pairings are tracked separately and can be reviewed later.
