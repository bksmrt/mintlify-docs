When Proof detects a new regulatory change, your institution must acknowledge it, review what it affects, and record how you addressed it. This guide walks you through the full response from first sighting to final resolution.

## Before you start

- You need the institutional admin or compliance officer role to acknowledge and resolve a change. A consulting firm acting for your college can raise a change, but the response is the college's.
- The change must appear in the **Detected Updates** or **Regulatory Changes** tab of the **Regulatory Intelligence** page. If it is not visible, check the **Corpus freshness** strip at the top of the page for failing sources.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Regulatory Intelligence page">
    Click **Regulatory Intelligence** in the sidebar. The page opens on the **Needs Attention** tab, which surfaces urgent items. Switch to the **Regulatory Changes** tab to see the full register, or to the **Detected Updates** tab to review newly detected entries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acknowledge the change">
    Find the change you want to work. Choose **Acknowledge** from its row menu, or open it and click **Acknowledge**. Acknowledging records that the change has been read and is under consideration. It does not resolve it. The first acknowledgement is kept: if a colleague acknowledges at the same moment, the record keeps whoever was first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the impact analysis">
    Click the change title to open its detail page. Click the **Impact** tab to see what the detection engine matched against your compliance rules and policies. Each match shows a confidence score. Use the filters (**All**, **Rules**, **Policies**, **High confidence**, **Medium confidence**, **Low confidence**) to narrow the results. Click any match row to expand the reasoning and evidence. The **Affected Items** tab lists the affected rules and policies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Propose a project if the change needs work">
    On the **Detected Updates** tab, click **Create Project** on a major entry, or **Override ↑** on any other entry and explain why. Proof suggests an owner and shows an impact summary. Click **Submit for approval**. The project goes to your college's approval before its work starts, and a message says who it is waiting on.

    From a change's detail page, you can also click **New Project** to start a project with the change attached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve the change">
    When you are ready to close the loop, click **Resolve Change** on the detail page. Choose a resolution type:

    - **Policy Updated**: your affected policies and procedures have been updated.
    - **No Action Needed**: after review, no updates are required.
    - **Exception Requested**: an exception is pending approval.

    Enter notes that describe what you did. Click **Submit Resolution** to finalize.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The change status moves to **Resolved** and a timestamp is recorded. Who acknowledged and resolved the change, and when, cannot be edited later.
- The resolution type, notes, and user are added to the **History** tab of the change record.
- If you proposed a project, find it under **Approvals** until it is approved, then in **Projects**.

<Tip>
  To see how quickly your college responds to change, open **Regulatory change response** in Report Studio, under **What changed**. It lists what still waits for acknowledgement, oldest first, and each change from the last 12 months: who acknowledged and resolved it, how many days it took, and its response project.
</Tip>
