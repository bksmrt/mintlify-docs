This guide shows you how to revise an existing institutional policy, record the change, and advance it through review to publication.

## Before you start

- You need a role that can edit policies. If you do not see **Edit** in the row menu, ask your institutional administrator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the policy in edit mode">
    Go to [**Policy Inventory**](/user-guide/compliance/policy-inventory). Find the policy and choose **Edit** from the row menu. The policy opens in the document editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update the **Title**, **Category**, **Summary**, or **Content** as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status">
    Use the **Status** dropdown to move the policy along: **Draft**, then **Review** when it is ready for stakeholders, **Approved** when reviewers sign off, and **Published** once it is live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Click **Cancel** to discard your changes instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm linked standards">
    Switch to the **Related** tab on the policy detail page. Review each linked standard. Click **Confirm** if the policy still satisfies the standard, or **Flag** if it does not. Flagging opens a compliance gap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The policy history records every change and each new edition.
- Linked standards with confirmed reviews have their review clocks restarted.
- Flagged standards create gaps that appear in your compliance register.
- The policy appears on the **Review Calendar** according to its next review date.

<Tip>
  If a policy is under review currency, the **Related** tab shows a **Review Standing** rail on the right. It lists which standards are current and which need attention.
</Tip>
