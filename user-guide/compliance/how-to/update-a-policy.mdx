This guide shows you how to revise an existing institutional policy, record the change, and advance it through review to publication.

## Before you start

- You need a role that can edit policies: institutional admin, compliance officer, department head, or someone an admin has granted policy editing by name. If you do not see **Edit** in the row menu, ask your institutional admin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the policy in edit mode">
    Go to [**Policy Inventory**](/user-guide/compliance/policy-inventory). Find the policy and choose **Edit** from the row menu. The policy opens in the document viewer in edit mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update the **Title**, **Category**, **Summary**, or **Content** as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status">
    Use the **Status** dropdown to move the policy along: **Draft**, then **Review** when it is ready for stakeholders, **Approved** when reviewers sign off, and **Published** once it is live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Click **Cancel** to discard your changes instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- Saving a change to the policy's words writes a new revision. The **History** tab lists it, and you can compare it with the revision before.
- A revision of the words counts as the policy's review. Every linked standard is marked reviewed in your name, and the byline reads **Reviewed** with the day, your name, and "revised".
- The policy's next review date moves to its next cycle, and the **Review Calendar** updates.

<Note>
You cannot type a review date into the policy. A review date is earned by recording a review: revising the words, or answering the review question on the **Compliance** tab. See [Complete a policy review](/user-guide/compliance/how-to/schedule-a-review).
</Note>

<Tip>
  A revision released by a review project counts as the policy's review too, the same as a direct revision.
</Tip>
