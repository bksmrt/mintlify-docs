This guide shows you how to revise an existing institutional policy, record the change, and advance it through review to publication.

## Before you start

- You need a role that can edit policies. If you do not see **Edit** in the row menu, ask your institutional administrator.
- Know whether the change is a minor edit or a full revision. A full revision creates a new edition and resets the review clock.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the policy">
    Go to **Policy Inventory**. Find the policy you want to update and click **View Policy** from the row menu. The policy detail page opens on the **Content** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter edit mode">
    Click **Edit** in the row menu (or use the **Edit** option from the policy card on the inventory page). The policy opens in the document editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update the title, code, category, summary, effective date, review date, or body content as needed. The editor auto-saves your work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the change type">
    When you finish editing, decide whether to save as a minor edit or create a new edition. A new edition is required when the policy substance changes and you want to restart the review cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Advance through review">
    If the policy status is **Draft**, move it to **Review** when it is ready for stakeholders. When reviewers approve, change the status to **Approved**, then to **Published** once it is live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm linked standards">
    Switch to the **Related** tab on the policy detail page. Review each linked standard. Click **Confirm** if the policy still satisfies the standard, or **Flag** if it does not. Flagging opens a compliance gap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The policy history records every change and each new edition.
- Linked standards with confirmed reviews have their review clocks restarted.
- Flagged standards create gaps that appear in your compliance register.
- The policy appears on the **Review Calendar** according to its next review date.

<Tip>
  If a policy is under review currency, the **Related** tab shows a **Review Standing** rail on the right. It lists which standards are current and which need attention.
</Tip>
