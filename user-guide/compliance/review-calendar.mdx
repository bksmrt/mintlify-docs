The Review Calendar gathers every time-sensitive compliance obligation into one view. Compliance officers and accreditation liaisons use it to see what is overdue, what is due this month or quarter, and what is coming next.

## How to get here

Click **Review Calendar** in the sidebar.

## What you see

### Month grid

A full month calendar dominates the page. Each cell is one day. Days outside the current month are dimmed. Past days are slightly faded. Today is highlighted.

On any day that has deadlines or reviews, small colored dots appear:

- **Reviews**: policy and accreditation reviews (dark)
- **Rule deadlines**: compliance rule due dates (amber)
- **Disclosures**: required disclosures (clay)
- **Regulatory**: regulatory change deadlines (sage)

Up to three events show by name. If more exist, a **+N more** line appears.

### Navigation

Above the grid:

- **‹** and **›** move to the previous or next month
- **Today** jumps back to the current month

The current month name is displayed between the arrows.

### Focus a day

Click any calendar day to focus it. The lists below the grid then scope to that day only. A focus line appears showing the selected date with a **Clear** button to remove the focus.

### Urgency filter

A filter bar above the grid lets you scope the lists by urgency:

- **All**: every upcoming item
- **Overdue**: items past their due date
- **This month**: items due in the current calendar month
- **This quarter**: items due in the current quarter

Each option shows a count.

### Kind filter

A row of subject chips lets you filter by event kind:

- **Reviews**
- **Rule deadlines**
- **Disclosures**
- **Regulatory**

Click a chip to include that kind; click again to remove it. Empty means all kinds.

### Lists below the grid

Two sections appear under the calendar:

- **Overdue**: items whose due date has passed. A warning note appears at the top of this section with the count.
- **Upcoming**: items due today or in the future, sorted by date.

Each list row shows:

- A colored dot for its kind
- The event title
- Its kind label and agency (if any)
- How soon it is due: "Due today", "Due tomorrow", "In N days", or "Nd overdue"

Click any row to open the relevant rule, policy, project, or review page.

### Deep links

The calendar supports URL deep links:

- A **?date=YYYY-MM-DD** link opens the calendar focused on that day
- A **?month=YYYY-MM** link opens the calendar at that month

## What you can do here

- **Browse by month**: use the arrows to move forward or backward through the calendar
- **Focus a day**: click a day cell to see only that day's items in the lists below
- **Filter by urgency**: choose All, Overdue, This month, or This quarter to control the list scope
- **Filter by kind**: toggle Reviews, Rule deadlines, Disclosures, or Regulatory to show only what you need
- **Open an item**: click any event chip in the calendar or any row in the list to go to its detail page
- **Clear focus**: click **Clear** on the focus line to return to the full urgency-scoped view
