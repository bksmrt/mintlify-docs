**Manage Compliance** is your college's side of standards delivery. It lists the agencies your college answers to, what it holds from each, and what has been delivered to it. Compliance officers and institutional admins use it to keep the college's copies of agency standards current and to acknowledge changes the agencies make.

Your college works from its own copies of each agency's standards. When an agency's standard changes and the change is delivered, your copy updates and waits for someone to acknowledge it. Nothing is edited on this page.

## How to get here

Click **Manage Compliance** in the sidebar, under **Administration**. Institutional admins and compliance officers see it.

## What you see

### Waiting note

When delivered changes wait on an acknowledgement, a note at the top says how many, for example "3 delivered changes wait on an acknowledgement". Click **Review** to open the **Deliveries** tab on those changes.

### Agencies tab

One row for each agency your college has confirmed. Each row shows:

- The agency's short name and full name
- What kind of agency it is (for example **Regional accreditor** or **Federal agency**), how many standards you hold from it, how many changes wait to be acknowledged, and how many standards are behind the catalog
- **Standing**: **To acknowledge**, **Behind**, or **Current**
- **Last delivery**: the date of the most recent delivery

If no agency is confirmed yet, the tab says so. Your college confirms the agencies it answers to during setup. Confirming an agency delivers its standards to your college at once.

### Deliveries tab

One row for each delivery to your college. Each row shows:

- The standard, or for a first delivery, how many standards arrived when your college confirmed the agency
- The version change, for example "version 3 to 4", and whether it was a major, minor, or trivial change
- Who delivered it, and who acknowledged it and when
- **Standing**: **To acknowledge**, **Acknowledged**, **Delivered**, or **Restored** (the platform operators put the standard back to the version before)
- **Delivered**: the delivery date

Click **Filters** to narrow the list by **Agency** or **Standing**.

## What you can do here

- **Open an agency**: click its row, or choose **Open** from its row menu. The agency's page lists your college's own copies of its standards on its **Standards** tab.
- **Sync now**: on an agency that is behind the catalog, if you can write compliance rules, choose **Sync now** from its row menu. Proof delivers the newer versions of the standards your college holds from that agency. The delivery appears on the **Deliveries** tab and waits to be acknowledged.
- **Acknowledge one change**: on a delivery that waits, choose **Acknowledge** from its row menu.
- **Acknowledge several at once**: on the **Deliveries** tab, turn on selection, tick the deliveries that wait, and click **Acknowledge** in the bar that appears.
- **Open the agency from a delivery**: choose **Open the agency** from a delivery's row menu.

## Our copy on a standard

Every standard's page shows **Our copy** on its **Overview** tab: the version your college holds, whether it is the catalog's current version, and when it was delivered and by whom.

When a delivered change waits, **Our copy** says what changed, for example "CCNE changed II-C on Nov 3, 2026 (v3 → v4 · minor)". Compliance officers and institutional admins see **Acknowledge** beside it. Anyone else reads "A compliance officer or institutional admin acknowledges it."

Acknowledging is recorded on the standard's **History** tab. It means your college has read the change. It does not approve anything.

<Tip>
Certification Studio also says when a delivered change waits, with links to the standard and to all deliveries, and shows your college's copy under each standard's row.
</Tip>
