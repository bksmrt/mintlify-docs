**Manage Compliance** is your college's side of standards delivery. It lists the agencies your college answers to, what it holds from each, what has been delivered to it, and the federal statutes that apply to it. Compliance officers and institutional admins use it to keep the college's copies of agency standards current and to acknowledge changes the agencies make.

Your college works from its own copies of each agency's standards. When an agency's standard changes and the change is delivered, your copy updates and waits for someone to acknowledge it. Nothing is edited on this page.

## How to get here

Click **Manage Compliance** in the sidebar, under **Administration**. Institutional admins and compliance officers see it.

## What you see

### Waiting note

When delivered changes wait on an acknowledgement, a note at the top says how many, for example "3 delivered changes wait on an acknowledgement". Click **Review** to open the **Deliveries** tab on those changes.

### Agencies tab

One row for each agency your college has confirmed. Each row shows:

- The agency's short name and full name
- What kind of agency it is (for example **Regional accreditor** or **Federal agency**), how many standards you hold from it, how many changes wait to be acknowledged, and how many standards are behind the catalog
- **Status**: **To acknowledge**, **Behind**, or **Current**
- **Last delivery**: the date of the most recent delivery

If no agency is confirmed yet, the tab says so. Your college confirms the agencies it answers to during setup. Confirming an agency delivers its standards to your college at once.

### Deliveries tab

One row for each delivery to your college. Each row shows:

- The standard, or for a first delivery, how many standards arrived when your college confirmed the agency
- The version change, for example "version 3 to 4", and whether it was a major, minor, or trivial change
- Who delivered it, and who acknowledged it and when
- **Status**: **To acknowledge**, **Acknowledged**, **Delivered**, or **Restored** (the platform operators put the standard back to the version before)
- **Delivered**: the delivery date

Click **Filters** to narrow the list by **Agency** or **Standing**.

### Federal statutes tab

One row for each federal statute that applies to your college. The list follows your college's **Institutional Profile**: whether it participates in Title IV, and its control (for example public or private nonprofit). Nobody picks a statute here. Change the profile and this list follows it. A statute the platform adds to its catalog arrives here the same way.

Each row shows:

- The statute's name and what it asks of the college, for example "Due Oct 1 each year", or **No filing date**
- **Status**: **Applies**, or **Exception granted** when an approved exception says it does not apply to your college until a day
- **Why it applies**: the fact on the profile that brings it in

When you edit Title IV participation or the control on the profile, the profile says which statutes saving it adds or takes off, before the save. Each change is written on the History. A statute taken off is kept, switched off, and comes back if the profile changes back.

To ask that a statute not apply to your college, request an exception on the [Exceptions](/user-guide/compliance/exceptions#when-a-federal-statute-does-not-apply) page.

## What you can do here

- **Open an agency**: click its row, or choose **Open** from its row menu. The agency's page lists your college's own copies of its standards on its **Standards** tab.
- **Sync now**: on an agency that is behind the catalog, if you can write compliance rules, choose **Sync now** from its row menu. Proof delivers the newer versions of the standards your college holds from that agency. The delivery appears on the **Deliveries** tab and waits to be acknowledged.
- **Acknowledge one change**: on a delivery that waits, choose **Acknowledge** from its row menu.
- **Acknowledge several at once**: on the **Deliveries** tab, choose **Select** from a waiting delivery's row menu, tick the others that wait, and click **Acknowledge** in the bar that appears.
- **Open the agency from a delivery**: choose **Open the agency** from a delivery's row menu.

## An agency's page

Open an agency from the **Agencies** tab to see its own page.

- **Standards** lists your college's own copies of the agency's standards. Under each copy, a line says when its words were last checked against the agency's text, for example "Checked against CCNE's text 12 Nov 2026", or "Not yet checked against CCNE's text".
- **Sources** lists the pages watched for changes to the agency's requirements. Each source shows when it was last checked, or **Paused**. Click the refresh button on a source to check it now.

### Report a requirement that looks wrong

If a copy's words don't match what the agency wrote, tell the platform's operators.

1. From the copy's **⋯** menu, choose **Report**. You can also do this from a requirement's row in Certification Studio.
2. Under **What did you notice?**, choose one or more reasons: **The words differ**, **A number is wrong**, **Something is missing**, **Not the current edition**, or **Other**. Add a note; **Other** needs one.
3. Click **Send**.

The row then reads **Sent for review**, with who sent it, for everyone at your college. Until the operators' nightly review reads it, the sender can click **Withdraw**. When it is checked, the sender gets a notice, and the row shows the outcome for 30 days: **Checked** (your copy reads as the agency wrote it), or **Corrected** (the reading was wrong and is being put right). If the agency itself changed its words, the new wording reaches your copy once approved, for you to acknowledge.

### Watch your own sources

If you can write compliance rules, you can add the college's own pages to watch for an agency. They are checked each night with the platform's sources, and new items arrive in [Regulatory Intelligence](/user-guide/compliance/regulatory-intelligence).

- **Add a source**: on the **Sources** tab, enter the page's web address (beginning `https://`) and click **Add source**.
- **Change a source**: from a source's **⋯** menu, choose **Edit address**, **Pause checking** (or **Resume checking**), or **Remove…**. Removing asks you to confirm.

Each change is written on the agency's **History**.

## Our copy on a standard

Every standard's page shows **Our copy** on its **Overview** tab: the version your college holds, whether it is the catalog's current version, and when it was delivered and by whom.

When a delivered change waits, **Our copy** says what changed, for example "CCNE changed II-C on Nov 3, 2026 (v3 → v4 · minor)". Compliance officers and institutional admins see **Acknowledge** beside it. Anyone else reads "A compliance officer or institutional admin acknowledges it."

Acknowledging is recorded on the standard's **History** tab. It means your college has read the change. It does not approve anything.

Wherever your college reads a standard's words, it reads its own copy: gaps, the compliance workspace, readings, searches, reports, deliverables, and the assistant. Where your college holds no copy of a standard, its words are the platform's, and a report such as **Obligations standing** says so: "the platform's wording, not yet delivered".

<Tip>
Certification Studio also says when a delivered change waits, with links to the standard and to all deliveries, and shows your college's copy under each standard's row.
</Tip>
