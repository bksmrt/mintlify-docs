Regulatory Intelligence is your institution's watch room for external rule changes. It collects updates from monitored sources, classifies each change by magnitude and confidence, and gives you a clear path from detection to action.

## How to get here

Click **Regulatory Intelligence** in the sidebar.

## What you see

The page opens as a journal with five leaf tabs across the top.

### Needs Attention

A curated feed of items that require a human decision. This includes detected changes that are not yet acknowledged, high-impact findings, and anything the system cannot auto-resolve.

### Regulatory Changes

A register of hand-entered or imported regulatory changes. Each row shows:

- **Change**: title and description
- **Status**: Detected, Under Review, Action Required, or Resolved
- **Effective**: the effective date
- A row menu with **View Details**, **View Source** (when a source link exists), and **Acknowledge** (until acknowledged)

Above the register, a stats line shows **Total**, **Detected**, **Action Required**, and **Resolved** counts. Click the notices link to see changes that need no action.

#### Filters

Click **Filters** to narrow by:

- **Status**: Detected, Under Review, Action Required, Resolved
- **Level**: Federal, State, Regional, Local, Institutional
- **Impact**: Critical, High, Medium, Low
- **Kind**: Findings (needs action) or Notices (no action needed)
- **Acknowledged**: yes or no
- **Record State**: draft, active, or archived

Click **Add Change** to create a new regulatory change manually.

### Detected Updates

A feed of automatically detected updates from monitored sources. Each entry shows:

- **New** tag if first seen within the last 72 hours
- **Curation status**: Pending Review, In Review, Approved, Published, or Archived
- **Entry type**: Document, Policy Update, Announcement, News, or Update
- **Source name**
- **Classification badge**: the system's assessed magnitude and confidence
- **Title**, **date**, and **description**

#### Actions per entry

- **Source**: open the original external link
- **Acknowledge**: mark the entry as reviewed (for minor or routine changes)
- **Dismiss**: archive the entry
- **Override ↑**: escalate the entry into a compliance project even if the system classified it as minor
- **Create Project**: for entries classified as major, create a project directly
- **Trace**: expand the entry to see detection reasoning, source type, content signature, and source URL

#### Classification review

Click the classification badge on any entry to review or override its magnitude. You can change it to minor, moderate, or major, and add a justification. Once an entry is approved, archived, or published, its classification is locked.

#### Feed filters

Filter detected updates by source, new only, status, and type. A search field also lets you filter by title or description.

#### Policy linking banner

If your institution has not yet linked policies to compliance rules, a banner appears at the top of the feed. It guides you to:

1. Add policies in the Policy Inventory
2. Add compliance rules in the Compliance Registry
3. Link policies to rules

Until linking is complete, only document-type entries are shown.

### Control Health

Every monitored standard with its control health. Filter by status, sort by payoff, and accept or dismiss Proof's suggestions for each standard.

### Decay

How fresh your policies, evidence, and documents are, so you can see what is going stale.

### Corpus freshness strip

At the very top of the page, a trust layer shows how current the detection signal is. If catalog updates are available, a note appears with a link to the Sync Station.

## What you can do here

- **Acknowledge a minor change**: click **Acknowledge** on a detected update to record that it has been reviewed
- **Escalate to a project**: click **Override ↑** or **Create Project** to turn a change into a tracked compliance project with an owner and tasks
- **Classify manually**: click the classification badge to override the system's assessment
- **Dismiss noise**: click **Dismiss** to archive entries that do not require action
- **Add a manual change**: click **Add Change** on the Regulatory Changes tab to record a change the system did not detect
- **Trace provenance**: click **Trace** on any detected entry to see exactly how and when it was found
