The **Compliance calendar** is a delivered report that lists every dated obligation your college will be cited for missing, grouped by how soon it is due. Compliance officers use it to see what is overdue, who owns each date, and which yearly statute filings are done.

Where the [Review Calendar](/user-guide/compliance/review-calendar) is a month view for browsing, the Compliance calendar is a report: filtered, exported, saved, and shared like any other report.

## How to get here

Open **Report Studio** and choose **Compliance calendar** under **Standing**. The report's address is `/reports/compliance-calendar`.

The college's History readers, such as institutional admins and compliance officers, read the whole calendar. A department head reads her own departments' dates.

## What you see

The head says what the calendar covers, how many obligations are open, and how many are overdue. Figures under it count what is **Overdue**, **Due in 30 days**, due later in the window, and **Undated**.

Rows are grouped:

- **Overdue**: past its date, to a year back
- **Next 30 days**: due from today to 30 days out
- **31–90 days** (or the end of the window you chose): due after 30 days
- **Undated**: a regulatory change with no effective date yet

Each row shows the obligation, its kind, its owner, its due date, and its standing, for example **Tomorrow** or a number of days to or past the date. Click an obligation's name to open its record.

### Kinds of obligation

The calendar gathers:

- **Compliance item due**
- **Policy review** and **Standard sunset**
- **Submission deadline** for a review cycle
- **Regulatory change takes effect**, for changes not yet acknowledged
- **Exception expires**
- **Evidence goes stale**
- **Statute**: a yearly filing a statute your college holds requires by law
- **Program review**, **Course review**, and **Course leaves the catalog**, if your college has the Academic Operations module

### Filters

Click **Filters** to set:

- **Window**: the next 30, 90, 180, or 365 days. The default is 90.
- **Kind**: one kind, or every kind
- **Owner**: one person, or everyone
- **Department**: one department, or every department

## Mark a statute filed

A statute with a fixed yearly date, such as the Annual Security Report under the Clery Act, appears once a year as a **Statute** row. Its due date comes from the statute catalog. A line under the row says whether that year has been filed.

The statutes your college holds follow its Institutional Profile; [Manage Compliance](/user-guide/compliance/manage-compliance#federal-statutes-tab) lists them. A statute with an approved [exception](/user-guide/compliance/exceptions#when-a-federal-statute-does-not-apply) is off the calendar until the exception ends.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the statute's row">
    Look for the row of kind **Statute**. A year can be marked filed from 90 days before it is due. Before then, the line says "Can be marked filed from" and the date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Mark filed…** on the line under the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the day">
    In **Filed on**, choose the day it was filed. You cannot choose a day in the future. Optionally, search for and link what was filed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Mark filed**. The row's standing reads **Filed** and the day, or **Filed** with how many days late. The line under it says who filed it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To correct the day, click **Change…** on the line, choose the right day, and click **Change the day**. The linked document is kept unless you change it.

<Note>
Compliance officers and institutional admins mark a statute filed. Each filing and correction is recorded on the History. A filed year is listed but no longer counted as open.
</Note>

## Export, save, and share

The report's foot offers **Export** as **CSV**, **Excel**, or **PDF**. You can save a snapshot of the report, save it as a named report, share it, and schedule it, as with any report in Report Studio.
