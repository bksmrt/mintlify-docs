The Compliance Registry is where your institution tracks every rule it is held to, from federal law down to internal policy. Compliance officers and accreditation liaisons use it to see what is in force, what is due, and where gaps exist.

## How to get here

Open **Compliance Rules** in the sidebar, then choose a level: **Federal**, **State**, **Regional**, **Local**, or **Institutional**.

## What you see

### Register list

Each level opens a journal-style register that lists every rule for that jurisdiction. Every row shows:

- **Number**: the rule's position in the register
- **Standard, Citation and Subjects**: the rule title, its code, and any category tags
- **Standing**: whether the rule is in force, scheduled, expired, archived, or draft
- **Effective**: the effective date, or "Not set"
- **Actions menu** (the **⋯** button): open the rule detail, view it in Certification Studio, add a compliance item, or request an exception

Rows with open gaps or upcoming deadlines show indicator chips beneath the title so you can spot risk at a glance.

### Stats bar

At the top of the register:

- **Total Rules**: the full count for the selected level
- **Policy Gaps**: rules that have no linked policy response. Click this tile to filter the list to gaps only.

### Filters

Click **Filters** to narrow the register by:

- **Regulatory Body**: the agency or body that issued the rule
- **Category**: the rule's subject category
- **Risk Level**: critical, high, medium, low, or minimal
- **Record State**: draft, active, or archived
- **Policy Gaps Only**: toggle to show only rules without linked policies

### Search

Use the **Consult the register** search field to find rules by title or code.

### Pagination

The register paginates. Choose how many rows to show per page, then use **Prev** and **Next** to move through the list.

### Compliance Watch rail (desktop)

On wide screens, a sticky side rail called **Compliance Watch** stays in view. It shows:

- **Overdue** rule deadlines
- **Upcoming** deadlines
- **Open gaps** (rules without compliant coverage)

Each item in the rail is clickable and routes to the relevant rule or review.

## What you can do here

- **Open a rule**: from the row menu (**⋯**), choose **View details** to open the rule detail page
- **Expand a rule in place**: click the title to reveal its description, citation, and a ledger of compliance items beneath the row
- **Filter by gaps**: click the **Policy Gaps** stat tile to surface uncovered rules
- **Add a compliance item**: from the row menu, choose **Add compliance item**
- **Request an exception**: for rules that allow exceptions, choose **Request exception** from the row menu
- **View in Certification Studio**: from the row menu, open the rule in Certification Studio
