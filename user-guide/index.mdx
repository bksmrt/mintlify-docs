This guide is for institution staff: compliance officers, accreditation liaisons, and institutional admins. Each area has two kinds of pages:

- **Feature pages** explain a screen: how to find it, what you see, and what you can do there.
- **How-to pages** walk through a process step by step and link to each screen you use along the way.
