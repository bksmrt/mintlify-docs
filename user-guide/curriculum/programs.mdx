The Programs page lists your institution's academic programs. Use it to find a program, create one, or open its courses and history.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, open **Curriculum** and click **Programs**.

## What you see

### The list

Each row shows the program number (**№**), **Program**, **Status**, and **Credits**. Click a row to open the program.

### Search and filters

- Search with **Search name, code, description…**.
- Click **Filters** to narrow by **Degree type**, **Status**, or **Department**.
- Turn on **Sunset programs** to include programs that are no longer active.

### A program's page

A program has four tabs: **Overview**, **Courses**, **Related**, and **History**.

## What you can do here

- **Create a program**: click **New Program**. In **Create Academic Program**, fill in **Program Name**, **Program Code**, **Description**, **Credential Type**, **Department**, **CIP Code**, **Total Credits**, and **Expected Terms**, then click **Create Program**. This needs write access to curriculum.
- **Propose a program change**: from [**Courses**](/user-guide/curriculum/courses), open **Actions** and choose **Start proposal…**, then pick **New Program**, **Modify Program**, or **Discontinue Program (with teach-out)**.
