The Sections page is your **Schedule of Classes**. Use it to plan the sections offered in a term, assign instructors, and publish the schedule.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, open **Curriculum** and click **Sections**.

## What you see

### Term

The term picker in the header chooses which term you are working on. Pick a term first; most actions need one.

### The schedule

Each row shows the **Course**, **Section**, **Status**, **Location**, **Seats**, and **Faculty**. Switch between **Table view** and **Card view** under **Views**. Click a section to open its **Section detail**.

### Search and filters

- Search with **Search sections, courses, faculty, campus…**.
- Filter by status: **All**, **Draft**, **Pending**, **Approved**, **Scheduled**, **Published**, **Cancelled**, **Discarded**.
- Use **Filters** to narrow further, for example by campus.

## What you can do here

- **Add a section**: click **New section**. See [Schedule a section](/user-guide/curriculum/how-to/schedule-a-section).
- **Request a section** (faculty): faculty see **Request a section** instead of **New section**.
- **Copy last term's schedule**: open the **More actions** menu and choose **Roll forward**. See [Roll a schedule forward](/user-guide/curriculum/how-to/roll-forward-a-schedule).
- **Number sections**: choose **Re-process numbers** in **More actions** to assign 001, 002, 003… to every section of a course.
- **Export**: choose **Export**, then **CSV (.csv)** or **Excel (.xlsx)**.
- **Act on many sections**: select rows, then use **Publish**, **Cancel**, or **Assign instructor**. Click **Clear** to deselect.

<Note>
When you publish in bulk, Proof shows **Publish sections** with a summary first. Locked sections and sections with validation errors are skipped.
</Note>
