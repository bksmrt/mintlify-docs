The Sections page is your **Schedule of Classes**. Use it to plan the sections offered in a term, assign instructors, and publish the schedule.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, open **Curriculum** and click **Sections**.

## What you see

### Term

The term picker under the page's title chooses which term you are working on. Pick a term first; most actions need one.

### The schedule

Each row shows the course, **Section**, **Status**, **Seats**, **Location**, and **Faculty**. To switch between a table and cards, click the view button beside the search. Click a section to open its **Section detail**.

### Search, views, and filters

- Search with **Search sections, courses, faculty, campus**.
- Click the filter mark in the search box to open **Views & filters**. Under **Saved views**, apply a view you kept. Under **Filters**, pick one or more pills for **Status** (**Draft**, **Pending**, **Approved**, **Scheduled**, **Published**, **Cancelled**, **Discarded**) and **Campus**. With no pill picked, every status shows.
- In the same panel, turn on **Hide locked sections**, or **Show archived terms** to offer archived terms in the term picker.

## What you can do here

- **Add a section**: click **New section**. See [Schedule a section](/user-guide/curriculum/how-to/schedule-a-section).
- **Request a section** (faculty): faculty see **Request a section** instead of **New section**, and can choose **Request to teach** from a section's **⋯** menu.
- **Copy last term's schedule**: open **Actions** and choose **Roll forward**. See [Roll a schedule forward](/user-guide/curriculum/how-to/roll-forward-a-schedule).
- **Number sections**: choose **Re-process numbers** in **Actions** to assign 001, 002, 003… to every section of a course.
- **Export**: in **Actions**, choose **Export CSV** or **Export Excel**.
- **Act on many sections**: open a section's **⋯** menu and choose **Select**, or press and hold the row. Tick more sections, then use **Publish**, **Cancel**, or **Assign instructor**. Click **Clear** to untick all, or **Done** (or press Esc) to leave select mode.

<Note>
When you publish in bulk, Proof shows **Publish sections** with a summary first. Locked sections and sections with validation errors are skipped.
</Note>
