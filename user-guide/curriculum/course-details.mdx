The course page holds everything about one course. Use it to check catalog data, see linked programs and sections, link policies and compliance rules, and move the course through its status.

## How to get here

Open [**Courses**](/user-guide/curriculum/courses) and click a course.

## What you see

### Tabs

- **Overview**: identity (subject, course number, title, credits), description, delivery modes, instructional formats, grading basis, campuses, and lifecycle dates such as **Effective Date**, **Expiration Date**, **Last Review**, and **Next Review**
- **Related**: linked programs and other connected records
- **Sections**: sections offered for this course
- **Prerequisite**: the prerequisite tree
- **Outcomes**: the course's learning outcomes
- **Compliance**: linked compliance rules and policies
- **Financial**: funding details such as **Fund Code**
- **History**: every change to the course

### Side panel

The side panel shows **Course Information**: **Credit Hours**, **Contact Hours**, **Level**, **Status**, **Department**, **Category**, **Last Reviewed**, **Next Review Due**, **Catalog Year**, and **Linked Programs**. Under **Quick Actions** you find **Edit Course** and **Create Section**.

## What you can do here

- **Edit the course**: click **Edit Course**. This needs write access to curriculum.
- **Link a policy or rule**: on the **Compliance** tab, click **+ Link Policy** or **+ Link Rule**.
- **Move it through status**: the actions menu changes with the course's status:
  - **Submit for Approval** when it is a draft
  - **Recall to Draft** while it is in review
  - **Publish Course** once it is approved (approvers only)
- **Archive or delete**: choose **Archive Course** or **Delete Course** from the actions menu.
