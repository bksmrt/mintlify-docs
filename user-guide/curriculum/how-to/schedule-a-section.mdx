A section is one offering of a course in a term: its meetings, seats, and instructor. Sections move from **Draft** to **Pending** to **Scheduled** to **Published**.

## Before you start

- Institutional admins, department heads, and compliance officers can create, approve, and publish sections. Faculty can request one with **Request a section**.
- The course must already be in the catalog. See [Courses](/user-guide/curriculum/courses).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the section">
    Open [**Sections**](/user-guide/curriculum/sections), pick the term, and click **New section**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in each step">
    Work through **Basics**, **Meetings**, **Seats**, **Policies**, and **Notes**, then check the **Summary**. Click **Save draft** at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send for approval">
    Click **Send for approval**. Fix any blocking issues the page lists first; validation must pass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    A scheduler opens the section and clicks **Approve**. Proof shows **Section approved — ready to publish**. Until then, other users see **Awaiting scheduler approval**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    The scheduler clicks **Publish**. Proof shows **Section published**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The section appears as **Published** in the schedule.
- To change a published section, edit it and click **Re-publish**.
- To withdraw it, choose **Cancel section**. Proof shows what the cancellation affects before you confirm.
