Changes to an existing course, and retiring a course, go through approval like a new course. Reviewers see what changes side by side with the current values.

## Before you start

- You need write access to curriculum.
- To deactivate, know the last term the course will be offered.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the proposal">
    Open [**Courses**](/user-guide/curriculum/courses), click **Actions**, and choose **Start proposal…**. Choose **Modify Course** to change a course, or **Deactivate Course** to retire one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the course">
    In **Course being modified** (or **Course to deactivate**), pick the course.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    To modify: enter a **Justification for change**. Change **CIP code** only if it is changing; leave it blank to keep the current code. You edit the detailed values during authoring once the change is approved.

    To deactivate: enter **Last offered term**, **Deactivation effective date**, and **Reason for deactivation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the impacts (deactivate only)">
    Proof runs a where-used check and shows **What this would disturb**: programs and degree requirements that depend on the course. If there are hard impacts, check **I acknowledge the listed impacts and have a transition plan in place.** You cannot submit until you do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or submit">
    Click **Save draft**, or **Submit for approval**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The proposal lands in the [Approvals](/user-guide/approvals/approvals) queue.
- Approvers see a field-by-field diff of the change in their Inbox.
- If changes are requested, see [Resubmit after changes are requested](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/resubmit-after-changes).
