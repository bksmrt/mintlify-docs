Instead of building a term from scratch, copy last term's sections into it. Proof clones every section that was not cancelled, with its meetings and instructors, as drafts you can then adjust.

## Before you start

- You need to be a scheduler: institutional admin, department head, or compliance officer.
- The new term must exist and be selectable in the term picker.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the new term">
    Open [**Sections**](/user-guide/curriculum/sections) and choose the term you want to fill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Roll forward">
    Open the **More actions** menu and choose **Roll forward**. The **Roll forward from previous term** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the source term">
    In **Source term**, choose the term to copy from. Proof counts the eligible sections and warns you if the new term already has sections, or if some sections belong to archived courses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy">
    Click **Roll forward**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The copied sections appear in the new term as **Draft**.
- Review them, then number them with **Re-process numbers** if needed.
- Send them through approval and publish. See [Schedule a section](/user-guide/curriculum/how-to/schedule-a-section). You can publish many at once by selecting them and clicking **Publish**.
