New courses go through your institution's curriculum approval policy. The proposal asks only for enough to route and decide it. You fill in the full catalog details later, on the approved work project.

## Before you start

- You need write access to curriculum.
- Find the course in your state's course inventory. The proposal starts there.
- Have a justification ready: the need, how it fits your mission and accreditors, and any supporting evidence.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the proposal">
    Open [**Courses**](/user-guide/curriculum/courses), click **Actions**, and choose **Start proposal…**. In **Start a curriculum proposal**, choose **New Course**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the state inventory course">
    In **State inventory course**, pick the approved course from your state's inventory. This fills in **Subject code**, **Course number**, **Working title**, and **CIP code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the greenlight details">
    Under **Greenlight details**, complete the fields marked with *:

    - **Working title**: a working title is enough for now
    - **Owning department**
    - **CIP code**: checked against the national inventory when you submit
    - **Justification**

    Optionally choose a **Target term**. Leave it blank to let Proof infer it from the effective date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or submit">
    Click **Save draft** to finish later, or **Submit for approval** to route it now.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- Proof shows **Proposal submitted** and opens the [Approvals](/user-guide/approvals/approvals) queue on your proposal. Drafts also wait there until you submit them.
- Approvers get the step in their Inbox. See [Decide an approval](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/decide-an-approval).
- If an approver requests changes, see [Resubmit after changes are requested](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/resubmit-after-changes).
- Once approved, you author the full catalog details, such as description, credits, outcomes, and syllabus, on the work project.
