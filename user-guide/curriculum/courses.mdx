The Courses page is your institution's course catalog. Use it to find a course, add one, start a curriculum proposal, or manage catalog years.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, open **Curriculum** and click **Courses**. Curriculum is available to institutional admins, compliance officers, department heads, and faculty.

## What you see

### The catalog

Each row shows the **Course**, **Status**, credits (**Cr.**), and **Owner**. Click a row to open the course. See [Course details](/user-guide/curriculum/course-details).

### Search, views, and filters

- Search with **Search subject, number, title or CIP**.
- Click the filter mark in the search box to open **Views & filters** beside the list. Under **Saved views**, apply a view you kept. Under **Filters**, pick one or more pills for **Status** (**Published**, **Approved**, **In Review**, **Draft**, **Archived**), each with its count, and for **Level** (**Developmental**, **Introductory**, **Intermediate**, **Advanced**, **Graduate**, **Doctoral**). With no pill picked, every status or level shows.
- In the same panel, turn on **Include archived courses** to bring archived courses back into the list, or click **Hide demo data**.

### Catalog year

The year picker chooses which catalog year you are reading. Click **Compare** to compare it with another year, and pick the year in **Compare vs**. Turn on **Changed only** to show only the rows that differ, and click **×** beside the year to stop comparing.

## What you can do here

- **Add a course**: click **Add course**. This needs write access to curriculum.
- **Start a proposal**: open **Actions** and choose **Start proposal…**. See [Propose a new course](/user-guide/curriculum/how-to/propose-a-new-course).
- **Manage catalog years**: under **Catalog year** in the **Actions** menu, choose **Start new year…** (clone this year into a draft), **Publish year…** (dry-run check, then publish), or **Restore previous** (restore the live catalog to the previous publish).
- **Open related tools**: the **Actions** menu also links to **Outcomes Matrix** and **Exceptions**.
- **Act on many courses**: open a course's **⋯** menu and choose **Select**, or press and hold the row. Tick more courses, then use **Change status…**, **Pin**, or **Find & replace**. Click **Clear** to untick all, or **Done** (or press Esc) to leave select mode.
