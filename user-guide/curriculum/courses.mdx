The Courses page is your institution's course catalog. Use it to find a course, add one, start a curriculum proposal, or manage catalog years.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, open **Curriculum** and click **Courses**. Curriculum is available to institutional admins, compliance officers, department heads, and faculty.

## What you see

### The catalog

Each row shows the **Course**, credits (**Cr.**), **Status**, and **Owner**. Click a row to open the course. See [Course details](/user-guide/curriculum/course-details).

### Search and filters

- Search with **Search subject, number, title, or CIP…**.
- Click **Filters** to narrow by **Status** (**All**, **Published**, **Approved**, **In Review**, **Draft**, **Archived**) or **Level** (**All Levels**, **Developmental**, **Introductory**, **Intermediate**, **Advanced**, **Graduate**, **Doctoral**).

### Catalog year

The year picker in the header chooses which catalog year you are reading. Use **Compare** to compare it with another year. Turn on the option to show only rows that differ, and click **Stop comparing** when you are done.

## What you can do here

- **Add a course**: click **+ Add Course**. This needs write access to curriculum.
- **Start a proposal**: open **Actions** and choose **Start proposal…**. See [Propose a new course](/user-guide/curriculum/how-to/propose-a-new-course).
- **Manage catalog years**: under **Catalog year** in the **Actions** menu, choose **Start new year…** (clone this year into a draft), **Publish year…** (dry-run check, then publish), or **Roll back publish** (revert the live catalog to the previous publish).
- **Open related tools**: the **Actions** menu also links to **Outcomes Matrix** and **Exceptions**.
- **Change status in bulk**: click **Select**, choose courses, then pick a status from **Change status…**.
