A discussion is the comment thread on a record. You raise a question or a correction, @mention who should answer, and resolve the comment once it's settled. Everyone who can read the record can read its discussion.

## Where to find it

The **Discussion** drawer sits at the foot of these pages:

- A project, including its compliance workspace and its self-study document. These pages share the project's one thread.
- A course and a program.
- A saved report, if you own it or it was shared with you with **Can comment**.

The drawer's toggle row shows **Discussion** with the number of comments. Click it to open or close the thread.

## Post a comment

1. Type in **Comment, or @mention someone…** beside the toggle row. Type `@` to mention a person or a group.
2. Press **Ctrl+Enter** (**⌘+Enter** on a Mac) to post. **Enter** adds a new line.
3. Your comment waits in place for five seconds before Proof sends it, with **Sending in 5 s · nothing sent yet**. Click **Undo** in that time to take it back. Its words return to the box.

To answer a comment, click **Reply** on it. Replies and edits use the same keys.

If your seat on a project's committee is advisory, you can read the discussion but not post. The drawer says **Your seat is advisory: you read this discussion.**

## Work with comments

On each comment you can:

- **Reply** to it.
- **Resolve** it once it's answered, or **Reopen** it.
- **Edit** or **Delete** your own comment.

People who manage the discussion can also **Pin to Top** or **Unpin** a comment, **Highlight as Key Point** or **Remove Highlight**, and **Archive** or **Unarchive** it. On a project, these are the project owner, committee leads, institutional admins and compliance officers. On a course, a program, or a saved report, Proof decides who moderates from the record, for example the department heads it belongs to.

Above the thread, switch between **all**, **mine**, and **resolved** comments. Click the search icon to search the comments and filter by author or date. People who manage the discussion can also turn on **Archived** to include archived comments.

### Undo an action

After you resolve, reopen, edit, delete, pin, unpin, highlight, archive, or unarchive a comment, its place in the thread says what you did, with **Undo**. For example: **You resolved this comment**. Click **Undo** to reverse it.

**Undo** stays available until you close the drawer. If you delete your own comment, you have 10 minutes to restore it.

## Export a thread

People who manage the discussion see **Export** above the thread, with **CSV**, **Excel**, and **PDF**. Click a format to download the thread. These are project owners, committee leads, institutional admins and compliance officers. On a course or program, the people who moderate that record's thread can export it.

## See the discussion in a record's History

On a course, a program, a policy, a document, or a compliance rule, the **History** tab has a **Discussion** switch beside the filters, with the thread's comment count. Turn it on to show the record's comments among its changes, in date order.

On a course or a program, a comment in the History has **Open in the discussion**. Click it to open the drawer at that comment.
