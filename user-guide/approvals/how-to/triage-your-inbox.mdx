A quick daily pass through your Inbox keeps deadlines and approvals from slipping. This guide shows you a simple routine.

## Before you start

- The Inbox is available to institutional admins, compliance officers, and faculty.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with what needs action">
    Open [**Inbox**](/user-guide/approvals/inbox). In the **Docket**, click **Action required**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide approvals">
    Click **Approvals** in the Docket and work each decision card. See [Decide an approval](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/decide-an-approval).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open and act">
    Click a notice to read it, then click the main action button to open the related record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set aside what can wait">
    From the actions menu, choose **Snooze** and pick **1 hour**, **4 hours**, **Tomorrow**, or **Next week**. The notice returns when the time is up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the rest">
    Switch to **Unread** and click **Mark all read**, or click **Archive all**. To clear only some notices, click **Select**, choose them, and use **Mark read**, **Archive**, or **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- Snoozed notices wait in **Snoozed** until their time comes.
- Archived notices stay in **Archived**, so you can find them later.
