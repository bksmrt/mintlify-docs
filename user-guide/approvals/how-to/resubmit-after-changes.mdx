When an approver requests changes, the proposal comes back to you. This guide shows you how to find it, fix it, and send it back.

## Before you start

- You must be the author: the person who submitted it, its current owner, or its creator.
- Read the approver's reason. Proof always records one when changes are requested.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the returned proposal">
    You get a **Changes requested** notice in your [**Inbox**](/user-guide/approvals/inbox). You can also open [**Approvals**](/user-guide/approvals/approvals) and choose **Your move** under **Filters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the reason">
    Choose **History** from the row menu to read the approver's reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the proposal">
    From the row menu, choose **Edit & resubmit**. The proposal editor opens with a note at the top showing who asked for changes and what they wrote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resubmit">
    Update the **Greenlight details**. Click **Save changes** to keep working later, or **Resubmit for approval** to send it back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- Proof shows **Proposal resubmitted**. It is routed again as a new approval cycle, and the chain runs again from the start.
- The proposal leaves **With the author**, and approvers get the step again in their Inbox.

<Note>
  While a proposal is in approval, you cannot edit it. Recalling it closes the proposal, and you would need to file a new one.
</Note>
