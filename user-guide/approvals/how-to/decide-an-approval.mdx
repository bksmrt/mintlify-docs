When a colleague routes a proposal to you, the active step appears in your Inbox and on the Approvals page. This guide shows you how to record your decision.

## Before you start

- The step must be routed to you. If you do not see a decision card, someone else holds the current step.
- Have your reason ready if you plan to request changes or decline. Proof requires one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the proposal">
    Open [**Inbox**](/user-guide/approvals/inbox) and choose the **Approvals** view, or open [**Approvals**](/user-guide/approvals/approvals) and choose the **Your move** lane under **Filters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review what changes">
    Read the card. For curriculum changes, a field-by-field diff shows what the proposal changes. Click **Open project** to see the full record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your decision">
    Click one of the three buttons:

    - **Approve**
    - **Request changes**
    - **Decline**

    When a committee signs, the buttons use the committee's own words. For example, a consensus committee sees **Endorse**, **Suggest revisions**, and **Raise a concern**. A parliamentary committee sees **Approve the motion**, **Request revisions**, and **Decline**. A consultative committee sees **Recommend adoption**, **Recommend with revisions**, and **Advise against**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your reason">
    Proof asks you to confirm, for example "Decline this proposal?". A reason of at least 10 characters is required to request changes or decline. It is also required to approve a proposal that carries items not in line, such as a non-compliance, an exception, or an override. Otherwise the reason is optional.

    The confirm button stays off until the reason meets the rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click the confirm button. It carries the same word as the button you chose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- **Approve**: the proposal moves to the next step, or is approved if yours was the last.
- **Request changes**: the proposal moves to **With the author**, and the author sees your reason.
- **Decline**: the proposal is declined, and the author sees your reason.
- Your decision is recorded on the **History** tab of the record.
