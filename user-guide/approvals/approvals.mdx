The Approvals page is your institution's queue of pending decisions. It lists every proposal waiting on a decision, from every area, and shows whose move each one is.

## How to get here

Click **Approvals** in the sidebar. The page is available to institutional admins, compliance officers, and department heads.

## What you see

### The queue

The header shows how many submissions are **In the queue**. Each row shows:

- **Proposal**: the name of the submission
- **Standing**: for example **Awaiting decision**, **Changes requested**, **Paused**, **Not routed**, **No one can decide**, **Approved**, **Declined**, **Recalled**, or **Expired**
- **Submitted**: when it was sent

### Whose move

Click **Filters** to choose a lane under **Whose move**. Each lane shows a count:

- **Your move**: you can decide it, or it came back to you as the author
- **With the author**: changes were requested and the author must resubmit
- **Waiting on others**: someone else holds the current step
- **Settled**: decided, recalled, or expired

When the queue spans more than one area, chips under the filter let you narrow by area.

### The record

Click a row to open its record. It has two tabs:

- **Decision**: what the proposal changes, the current step, the **Approval path**, **Progress so far**, and the decision card if it is your move
- **History**: every step and decision so far

## What you can do here

- **Decide**: on the **Decision** tab, approve, request changes, or decline. See [Decide an approval](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/decide-an-approval).
- **Edit and resubmit**: if you are the author and changes were requested, choose **Edit & resubmit** from the row menu. See [Resubmit after changes are requested](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/resubmit-after-changes).
- **Edit a draft**: if you are the author of an unsubmitted proposal, choose **Edit draft**.
- **Open the source**: the row menu links to the source, for example **Open project**, **Open proposal**, **Open section**, or **Open the workspace**.
- **Reopen a paused request**: choose **Open paused request**.
