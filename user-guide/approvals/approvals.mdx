The Approvals page is your institution's queue of pending decisions. It lists every proposal waiting on a decision, from every area, and shows whose move each one is.

## How to get here

Click **Approvals** in the sidebar. The page is available to institutional admins, compliance officers, department heads, and faculty. Deans, provosts, presidents, and board members see it too, for the steps routed to them. Faculty see it for the proposals they authored.

## What you see

### The title and its kinds

Under the masthead, the page's title names the kind of decision you're looking at: **All kinds**, or one kind such as **Course approval**. Click the title to choose another. The menu lists the kinds under five types (**Curriculum**, **Students**, **Compliance**, **Faculty**, and **Projects**), each with how many wait. See [What you decide here](#what-you-decide-here) for the kinds under each type.

Under the title, one sentence says how many decisions wait on you and how old the oldest is, for example "4 decisions wait on you, the oldest 12 days. 2 need you."

### Needs you

**Needs you** lists the decisions that need you most, worst first:

- **Overdue**: a decision on your move that has waited too long. One person's overdue decisions of one kind fold into one row, for example "5 course substitutions · Elena Ortiz".
- **Looser than required**: a narrative section whose sentences are looser than their rule.
- **A rule moves**: a rule exception that, if approved, moves a rule the college is held to.

Click **Open** to open that decision under its row. On a folded row, click **Open the** followed by the number to open the kind, where you can decide them together. If more than four need you, click **View** followed by the number of the rest.

### The register

Below, the decisions are listed in bands by type, for example "Compliance · 3 waiting on you". Each row shows:

- **Proposal**: the name of the submission, who sent it, and how long ago
- **Status**: for example **Awaiting decision**, **Changes requested**, **Paused**, **Not routed**, **No one can decide**, **Approved**, **Declined**, **Recalled**, or **Expired**
- **Submitted**: when it was sent

Use the search field to find proposals and people. Types with nothing waiting are named under the list, for example "Faculty, Projects: none waiting".

### Whose move

Click the filter icon at the right end of the search field to open **Views & filters**, and choose a lane under **Whose move**. The page opens on **Your move**. Each lane shows a count:

- **Your move**: you can decide it, or it came back to you as the author
- **With the author**: changes were requested and the author must resubmit
- **Waiting on others**: someone else holds the current step
- **Settled**: decided, recalled, or expired

When the queue spans more than one area, the panel also has an **Area** choice to narrow by area.

### The record

Click a row to open its record under the row. You see its status and, if it is your move, its decision card.

Click **History** at the foot to see **Progress so far**, the **Approval path**, and **The details** under the decision. Click **Hide history** to fold them away. Click **Open the record** to go to the record's own page. Click the row again to close it.

## What you decide here

Every decision on someone else's submission comes to this page, whatever area it's from. The same card appears in your [Inbox](/user-guide/approvals/inbox) **Approvals** view.

| Type | Kinds include |
| --- | --- |
| **Curriculum** | **Course approval**, **Program approval**, curriculum proposals, validation exemptions |
| **Students** | Course substitutions, course waivers, and other curriculum exceptions |
| **Compliance** | **Narrative section**, **Suggested change**, **Consulting firm's link**, **Rule exception** (including a federal statute that does not apply), **Ethos exception**, **Delay**, **Cause**, **Another look** |
| **Faculty** | **Faculty appointment** |
| **Projects** | New projects, including compliance projects, **Recall**, **Subproject**, a project's **Ethos exception**, **Objective suggestion** |

A few kinds ask their own question instead of approve or decline:

- **Cause**: *Is the cause resolved?* Asked of the person who agreed a gap's cause, before the gap closes. Answer **Yes**, or **No** with what remains. See [The cause question](/user-guide/accreditation/gaps#the-cause-question).
- **Another look**: *Is the cause still resolved?* Asked when a finding comes back under a resolved gap. See [Another look](/user-guide/accreditation/gaps#another-look).
- **Delay**: *Allow the delay?* Asked when a gap's corrective action runs past its grace. Choose a date in **Allow the delay until** and click **Approve**, or click **Request changes** to require action. See [When a corrective action runs late](/user-guide/accreditation/gaps#when-a-corrective-action-runs-late).
- **Ethos exception**: a department, or a project, asks to use a different value for an Ethos setting, such as how a project's committee decides. The dean of the department's college decides it, else an institutional admin. The asker never decides their own.
- **Objective suggestion**: someone suggests an objective for the strategic plan, under the objective it serves. An institutional admin clicks **Add as** followed by the next number to add it to the plan, **Merge into** to fold it into an existing objective, or **Decline**. **Undo** stays in place while the card is on screen.

Cause, Another look, and Delay rows show only to the people asked and those who can answer in their place.

## What you can do here

- **Decide**: in the opened record, approve, request changes, or decline. See [Decide an approval](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/decide-an-approval).
- **Decide several at once**: choose one kind in the title. In the **Your move** lane, some kinds show a selection strip when two or more rows can be decided together. Tick the rows, then click **Approve** or **Decline** and confirm. Decline asks for one reason, which Proof writes onto each row. Each row is still its own decision, with its own history entry and its own notice to whoever filed it. Rows that carry something not in line, such as a drift finding, an exemption, or a rule exception, are held back and decided on their own card.
- **See the history**: choose **History** from the row menu, or click **History** at the foot of the opened record.
- **See how long approvals take**: from **Actions**, choose **Approval cycle time report**.
- **Edit and resubmit**: if you are the author and changes were requested, choose **Edit & resubmit** from the row menu. See [Resubmit after changes are requested](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/resubmit-after-changes).
- **Edit a draft**: if you are the author of an unsubmitted proposal, choose **Edit draft**.
- **Open the source**: the row menu links to the source, for example **Open project**, **Open proposal**, **Open section**, or **Open the workspace**.
- **Reopen a paused request**: choose **Open paused request**.
- **Send a waiting compliance project**: a compliance project that was never routed, expired, or was recalled says it waits to be sent again. If you may send it, click **Submit for approval** on its card.
