The Inbox collects everything that needs your attention: mentions, replies, task assignments, due reminders, and approvals routed to you. Use it to see what waits on you and to set aside what can wait.

## How to get here

Click **Inbox** in the sidebar.

## What you see

### The title and its views

Under the masthead, the page's title names the view you're in. Click it to choose another:

- **All**
- **Unread**
- **Action required**
- **All approvals**, or one kind under **Approvals**: decisions waiting on you (see below)
- **Snoozed**
- **Archived**

Each view shows a count. A line under the title sums it up, for example "3 unread · 2 waiting on you · 4 approvals".

### Notice list

Notices are grouped by when they arrived: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This Week**, and **Earlier**. Each notice shows its type, subject, and when it was **Received**. A notice with a deadline also shows **Upcoming deadline**.

Notice types include **Mention**, **Reply**, **Assignment**, **Due Soon**, **Overdue**, **Approval**, **Approved**, **Declined**, **Changes requested**, **Approval expiring**, **Approval escalated**, **Recalled**, **Delegated to you**, **Exception decided**, **Evidence**, **Compliance**, **Regulatory**, and **System**.

Use the search field to find notices. To filter by type, such as **Mentions**, **Replies**, **Task Assignments**, **Due Reminders**, **Approvals**, **Standards**, or **Project Updates**, click the filter icon at the right end of the search field to open **Views & filters**, and choose a **Type**.

### Detail pane

Click a notice to read it beside the list. The main action button opens the related record. The arrow beside it opens the rest of the actions.

### Approvals view

The **Approvals** view shows each decision waiting on you, of every kind the [Approvals](/user-guide/approvals/approvals#what-you-decide-here) page holds: proposals and sections, exceptions, suggestions, and the questions Proof asks about gaps, such as **Cause** (*Is the cause resolved?*), **Another look**, and **Delay** (*Allow the delay?*).

It reads like the Approvals page: one sentence of what waits on you, **Needs you**, and the decisions in bands by type. Click a row to open its decision under it and decide in place. Curriculum changes show a field-by-field diff of what changed. If nothing is waiting, you see **Nothing is waiting on you.**

To narrow the view by kind, choose the kind under **Approvals** in the title's menu. To narrow it by record, such as a project, program, or standard, open **Views & filters** and choose a **Record**.

## What you can do here

- **Open the record**: click the main action on a notice. See [Triage your inbox](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/triage-your-inbox).
- **Decide an approval**: open the **Approvals** view, open the decision under its row, and use its decision card. See [Decide an approval](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/decide-an-approval).
- **Snooze a notice**: from the actions menu, choose **Snooze**, then **1 hour**, **4 hours**, **Tomorrow**, or **Next week**. Snoozed notices move to **Snoozed**. Choose **Unsnooze** to bring one back.
- **Pin, mark read, archive, or delete**: use the actions menu on a notice.
- **Clear the list**: from **Actions**, choose **Mark all read** or **Archive all**.
- **Act on many at once**: choose **Select** from a notice's **⋯** menu, tick more notices (or click **Select all**), then use **Mark read**, **Archive**, or **Delete** in the bar that appears.
