The Inbox collects everything that needs your attention: mentions, replies, task assignments, due reminders, and approvals routed to you. Use it to see what waits on you and to set aside what can wait.

## How to get here

Click **Inbox** in the sidebar.

## What you see

### Notice list

Notices are grouped by when they arrived: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This Week**, and **Earlier**. Each notice shows its type, subject, and when it was **Received**. A notice with a deadline also shows **Upcoming deadline**.

Notice types include **Mention**, **Reply**, **Assignment**, **Due Soon**, **Overdue**, **Approval**, **Approved**, **Declined**, **Changes requested**, **Delegated to you**, **Evidence**, **Compliance**, **Regulatory**, and **System**.

### Detail pane

Click a notice to read it in the middle pane. The main action button opens the related record. The arrow beside it opens the rest of the actions.

### The Docket

The left column, called the **Docket**, holds your views. Each view shows a count:

- **All**
- **Unread**
- **Action required**
- **Approvals**: decisions waiting on you (see below)
- **Snoozed**
- **Archived**

Under the views, filter by type, such as **Mentions**, **Replies**, **Task Assignments**, **Due Reminders**, **Approvals**, **Standards**, or **Project Updates**. Click **All types** to clear the filter.

On desktop, collapse or expand the Docket with the arrow at its top.

### Approvals view

The **Approvals** view shows each approval step you can decide. Each card shows the project or course, its phase and status, and an **Open project** button. Curriculum changes show a field-by-field diff of what changed. If nothing is waiting, you see **No approvals waiting on you**.

Click **Filters** to narrow the view by **Kind**, such as **Course change** or **Rule exception**, or by **Record**, such as a project or program.

## What you can do here

- **Open the record**: click the main action on a notice. See [Triage your inbox](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/triage-your-inbox).
- **Decide an approval**: open the **Approvals** view and use the decision card. See [Decide an approval](/user-guide/approvals/how-to/decide-an-approval).
- **Snooze a notice**: from the actions menu, choose **Snooze**, then **1 hour**, **4 hours**, **Tomorrow**, or **Next week**. Snoozed notices move to **Snoozed**. Choose **Unsnooze** to bring one back.
- **Pin, mark read, archive, or delete**: use the actions menu on a notice.
- **Clear the list**: click **Mark all read** or **Archive all**.
- **Act on many at once**: click **Select**, choose notices (or **Select all**), then use **Mark read**, **Archive**, or **Delete**.
