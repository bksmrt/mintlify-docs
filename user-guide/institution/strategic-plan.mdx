The **Strategic Plan** section of your **Institution Profile** holds your college's plan and its numbered objectives. Projects and program reviews can then say which objective they serve, and the **Strategic plan alignment** report shows the work behind each one.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, click **Institution Profile** and scroll to **Strategic Plan**.

## What you see

- The plan's name, who adopted it and when, its years, and a link to the plan's document
- **The plan's objectives**, numbered, each with its goal and what it holds the college to
- **Work naming it**: how many projects and program reviews serve each objective. Click it to open the [Strategic plan alignment](/user-guide/reports/delivered-reports) report for that objective.
- **Ethos asks:** whether your [Ethos](/user-guide/institution/ethos) requires new work to name an objective, suggests it, or does not ask

## Keep the plan

Institutional admins edit the plan with the rest of the profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit state">
    Click **Edit Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the plan and add objectives">
    Fill in **The plan's name**, **Adopted by**, **Adopted on**, **First year**, and **Last year**. Click **+ Add an objective** for each objective. You can move, edit, or remove objectives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read objectives from a document (optional)">
    **link** the plan's document if it is already in Proof, or **upload** it. In **Read objectives from a document**, check the objectives to keep. Nothing is added until you check them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. An objective you removed is archived if work names it, and deleted if none does.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Suggest an objective

Staff who can open the profile, such as compliance officers, can suggest an objective. Institutional admins edit the plan directly instead.

1. Click **+ Suggest an objective**.
2. Enter **The objective**, what it means, and optionally its goal.
3. In **Serves**, choose the plan's objective it advances. This is required.
4. Click **Suggest**.

Your suggestions are listed under **Your suggestions** with their standing. Click **Withdraw** to take back one that is still waiting.

## Decide a suggestion

Suggestions from colleagues and from a consulting firm arrive on the profile and in [Approvals](/user-guide/approvals/approvals). Each card says who suggested it and **Suggested under** the objective it serves. Institutional admins decide:

- **Add as** the next number, to add it to the plan
- **Merge into** an existing objective, to record which objective it became without changing any words
- **Decline**, with an optional reason

**Undo** stays on the card while it is on screen.

## Name the objective a piece of work serves

When you start a project, a curriculum proposal, a policy review, or a program review's new cycle, Proof shows **Strategic objective it serves** if your Ethos asks for it. Proof suggests the likeliest objective. When the Ethos requires it, you cannot continue without one; when it only suggests it, the field is marked **(Optional)** and offers **None**.

On a project's page, the objective appears in the masthead. If you can edit the project, click it to change it.
