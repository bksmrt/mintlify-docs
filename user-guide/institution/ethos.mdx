Your Ethos is your college's posture: how it leans on operations and on students. The posture sets many defaults across Proof, such as how approvals route, how long evidence stays current, and how Readiness is weighted. You can adjust each setting within its range.

## How to get here

Open **Institution Profile** in the sidebar. Under the Ethos, click **Adjust →**, or **Set your ethos →** if no posture is set yet.

Everyone can read the Ethos. Only an institutional admin sets it or changes its settings.

## Choose a posture

A posture is one choice on each of two axes:

- **Operations axis**: **Balanced**, **Governance**, **Compliance**, **Innovation**, **Stewardship**, or **Agility**
- **Student axis**: **Balanced**, **Success**, **Access**, **Rigor**, **Formation**, or **Inclusion**

**Balanced** leaves an axis at the default. With Balanced on both axes, Readiness uses the default weights.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick directly or walk through">
    Select one preset on each axis. As you pick, **Effective ICI weights** shows how the posture would weight Readiness. If you are not sure, click **Not sure where to start?** to walk through five real situations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk through five moments (optional)">
    For each moment, choose what your college would do. Then click **Land the recommendation** and **See the recommended posture**. Proof recommends a posture and says why, using your answers and your college's own records. Click **Everything we looked at** to see the full list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save as new version**. Each save is a new Ethos version, recorded on the History.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you set some settings yourself, changing the posture asks **Keep the settings you set yourself?** Choose **Keep mine** or **Clear them**.

<Note>
If your college's records point to a different posture than the one you saved, a notice appears at the top of the Ethos page. Nothing changes until you save.
</Note>

<Tip>
With **Handwritten first pass** turned on in your profile (**Profile** > **Appearance** > **Handwriting**), the five moments are asked one at a time on a full page, and you can answer in your own words.
</Tip>

## Ethos configuration

At the foot of the Ethos page, click **Open Ethos configuration**. This page lists **What your Ethos sets**: every setting, grouped, with its **Value** and its **Exceptions**.

- A setting you changed is marked **Changed**. Its posture value and its range show under the value.
- A setting that affects the scores says so, for example **Moves Institutional Readiness only, never the Proof Standard.**

### Change a setting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit state">
    Click **Edit settings**. Each value becomes a control you can change in place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change values">
    Enter a value inside the setting's range. A bar at the foot counts your changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a reason and save">
    In **Why (recorded on the History)**, type at least 10 characters. Click **Save**, or **Discard** to drop the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also set a department's own value for a setting, for an academic year. See [Ethos exceptions](/user-guide/institution/ethos-exceptions).

## The Ethos assistant

On Ethos configuration, click the assistant's key beside **Edit settings**. Its menu offers **Walk me through my settings**, **What does my posture change?**, **Who may ask for an exception?**, and **Ask anything**.

The assistant's sheet has two views:

- **Steps** walks through the settings a group at a time. It shows what stands and sets nothing. Click **Show on the page** to jump to a setting's row. Use **Next →** and **← Back** to move.
- **Chat** answers questions about your settings. When it proposes changes, it draws them as the page's own controls. Click **Set these in the form** to fill them into the page. Nothing is saved until you save it there.

The assistant only proposes. It never saves a setting. Your institutional admin can turn it off in [Settings › Assistants](/user-guide/institution/assistants).
