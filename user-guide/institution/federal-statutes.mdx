Your college's federal statutes follow its **Institution Profile**. Whether the college takes part in Title IV, and what kind of college it is, decide which statutes govern it. You don't add or remove statutes one by one.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, click **Institution Profile**. Institutional admins edit the profile.

## Change what the college holds

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit state">
    Click **Edit Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the fields that decide statutes">
    Turn **Title IV Participant** on or off, or change **Control Type** or **Institution Type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read what the save will do">
    Before you save, the profile says which statutes change. For example, **Saving adds 2 federal statutes**, with their names, or **Saving takes 1 federal statute off**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- A statute the save adds brings its filing dates onto the [Compliance calendar](/user-guide/compliance/compliance-calendar).
- A statute the save takes off is kept, switched off. It comes back if the profile changes back.
- Each change is written on the college's History.
- [Manage Compliance](/user-guide/compliance/manage-compliance) lists the statutes that apply under **Federal statutes**, and why each one applies.
- When the platform adds a statute to its catalog, it reaches every college it applies to.

<Note>
If a statute applies to your college but should not for a time, a department can ask for an exception on the [Exceptions](/user-guide/compliance/exceptions) page. While the exception stands, the statute is off the Compliance calendar and your college's score.
</Note>
