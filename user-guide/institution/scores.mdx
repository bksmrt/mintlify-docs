Proof reads your college's compliance as two numbers built from the same six factors:

- **Readiness** is your college's own number. Your [Ethos](/user-guide/institution/ethos) weights it, so it reflects what your college values. It stays inside the college.
- **Proof Standard** is the external number. It always uses the same fixed weights, so it can be compared across colleges. Your Ethos, project scope, and exceptions cannot move it. Peers are ranked on this number, and term certificates are stamped from it.

## The six factors

| Factor | What it reads |
|---|---|
| **Obligation Coverage** | Mapped standards with a control |
| **Evidence Coverage** | Required evidence slots with attached proof |
| **Policy Freshness** | Policies inside their review cadence |
| **Execution Traceability** | Narrative sections approved, their notes resolved |
| **Governance Integrity** | Governance records kept, agency deadlines met |
| **Control Redundancy** | A second reviewer on the work, tasks done |

## On the dashboard

The ring at the top of the dashboard shows **Readiness**, or **Not yet assessed** if no work has been scored yet. Under it, one sentence says where your college stands for certification. Click **View more** to open the full index.

Open **Compliance Cadence** below the ring to see:

- **This Week**: this week's score and its trend
- **Current term**: the **Proof Standard so far** for the term in progress, and how many requirements have evidence requested
- **Track Record**: how many recent terms certified, and the best medal held
- **Peak**: the highest certified term

## Term certification

Each academic term is judged on its own when it ends. A term certifies when two things are true at its last week:

- The college has a Proof Standard.
- Evidence has been requested on at least half the requirements the college is held to.

The dashboard says how close you are, for example: *Evidence requested on 12 of 40 requirements. 20 are needed for Fall 2026 to certify.*

A certificate carries the term's Proof Standard and a medal: **Bronze** from 70, **Silver** from 80, and **Gold** from 90. A certificate under 70 carries no medal. Readiness never wears a medal.

Your standing reads one of:

- **Not yet assessed**: no work has been scored yet
- **Not certified**: no term has certified yet, with when the current term certifies
- **Certified**: with the latest certificate's score, medal, and term
- **No certified score**: the last two terms ended without a certificate

## The Compliance Index page

In the sidebar, click **Compliance Index**, or click **View more** on the dashboard. Institutional admins and compliance officers can open it.

- **Institutional Readiness**: the ring, with each factor's share and the standing sentence under it
- **Proof Standard**: the number, its methodology version, when it was assessed, and what it rests on. While a term runs, it says what a certificate would read today, for example *A Fall 2026 certificate would read 76 · Bronze today. Silver from 80.*
- A factor table that shows **Your weight** and **Adds to Readiness** beside **Standard weight** and **Adds to Standard**
- **Weekly trend** and **Historical trajectory** of both numbers. A change that moved Readiness without any work, such as a new Ethos version or a change in method, is marked on the trend.
- **Interactive simulator**, **Peer benchmark**, and **Recalculation history**

<Note>
In the week before a change in how the index is read, the dashboard and the Compliance Index page show a notice with today's number and the number read the new way. For example: from Monday 30 November, your index reads requirement by requirement, across every agency you hold, rather than your projects averaged. Nothing you did moves it. Click **What moved it →** to see each factor.
</Note>
