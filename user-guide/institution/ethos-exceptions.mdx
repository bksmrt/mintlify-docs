Every department and project follows your college's Ethos by default. Your institutional admin decides who may ask to differ, setting by setting. An exception changes nothing until it is asked for and approved.

## Decide who may ask

Institutional admins set this on **Ethos configuration**. See [Institutional Ethos](/user-guide/institution/ethos) for how to get there.

1. Find the **Exceptions** block at the top of the settings list. Its one switch turns exceptions on or off for the whole college. When it is off, no one may ask and no exception can be granted.
2. Under the switch, choose **Departments**, **Projects**, or both.
3. On each group of settings, and on each setting, use the small toggle to allow asks there. The **Exceptions** column on each row shows what is allowed.
4. Allowed means everyone. To leave someone out, type in **Leave out a department** or **Leave out a kind of project** and pick from the list.

Each change saves at once, with **Undo**. If a change would leave out a department or project with an exception already in effect, Proof asks once what should happen to it.

## Ask for a department exception

The department's chair, or the head of its linked unit, asks from the department's page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the department's settings">
    Open the department, go to its **Compliance** tab, and find **Settings from Ethos**. Each row shows the setting and whether the department follows **As the college** or holds an exception.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask">
    On the row, click **Ask for an exception**. Enter the value in **Ask for**, which must be inside the setting's range. Choose the year in **For the academic year**: this one or the next.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say why and send">
    In **Why (the deciders read it)**, type at least 10 characters, and click **Ask**. The row reads **Asked · waiting for a decision**. Click **Withdraw** to take it back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The ask goes to [Approvals](/user-guide/approvals/approvals). Proof tells you who decides it as you ask. Until it is decided, the college's value applies. Once approved, the department's row reads **Exception** with its academic year, and that value applies at once.

### Renew an exception

An exception runs to the last day of its academic year. Thirty days before, the department is told once. Click **Renew for** the next year on the row to ask again. The current exception holds until the renewal is decided.

### Set a department's value directly

Whoever decides a department's exceptions can also set its value without an ask, for example when it was agreed outside Proof. Click **Set…** on the department's row, or use **Departments you decide** on Ethos configuration. Enter the value, the academic year, and a reason, then click **Set**. It takes effect at once, and the department's chair and head are told.

## Ask for a project exception

When you create a project, the **Governance model** step shows the committee model your Ethos sets for it. To use another model for that project, click **Ask for a different model…**, choose the model, and say why. The ask goes to Approvals. If approved before the committee's first vote, the project uses the model you asked for.
