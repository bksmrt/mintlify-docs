**Settings** > **Assistants** lists what runs on its own for your college, and what helps on a page when someone asks. An assistant only proposes: nothing it suggests counts until a person confirms it. Every run is kept on the record's History.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, click **Settings**, then **Assistants**. Institutional admins and compliance officers can open Settings.

## What you see

Each row shows:

- **Assistant**: its name and what it does
- **On**: its switch, or **Always on**
- **Last ran**: when it last ran
- **Waiting**: what waits on a person, or **Nothing waits on a person**
- **Decided in**: where its suggestions are decided, often as a link

The list depends on your college's modules.

| Assistant | What it does | Where you find it |
|---|---|---|
| **Mapping assistant** | Reads your policies against an agency's requirements and suggests an owner for each, with the sentence it rests on. The same switch covers **Suggest standards** on a course. | **Compliance Mapping** in Certification Studio |
| **Continuous monitoring** | Reopens evidence that aged past its shelf life or whose standard moved. Runs without a model, and is always on. | Each project's **Evidence** tab |
| **Ethos assistant** | Answers questions about your Ethos settings and who may ask for an exception, and proposes changes for you to set. | **Ethos configuration** |
| **Workspace assistant** | Drafts a requirement's sentence from your answers and answers questions. A draft is placed only once a linked record shows it. The same switch covers **Rewrite** on a passage, a finding, or a policy. | The compliance workspace |
| **Reports assistant** | Answers a question with one of your reports, read by your own access, and builds a custom report from what you ask. The same switch covers asking from the search box. | **Report Studio** |
| **Record assistant** | Says what needs you on the record you are reading, from the record itself. Off, each page keeps its own side panel. | The **p.** in the header, on a policy's page |
| **Outcome suggester** | Suggests learning outcomes for a course, for a person to accept. Academic Operations only. | On a course page |
| **Catalog copy editor** | Reads a course's catalog copy against your style and suggests fixes. Academic Operations only. | On a course page |
| **Scheduling assistant** | The room, meeting pattern, and workload advisors. Each answers a scheduler's questions about the choices ranked beside it. Off, the ranked choices stay. Academic Operations only. | A section's rooms and meeting patterns, and Workload Studio |
| **Student suggestions assistant** | Answers a student's questions about the sections ranked for them. Off, a student still sees the ranked sections. Academic Operations only. | A student's course suggestions |

An assistant that covers more than one feature says so under its name, for example **Also Rewrite on a passage, a finding or a policy: the same switch**.

Turning off the **Reports assistant** also keeps your college's records out of a consulting firm's answers across its engagements. The firm's answer names the colleges it did not read.

## Open a page's assistant

On a page that has an assistant, such as a policy, the compliance workspace, Certification Studio, or Ethos configuration, click the **p.** in the header, beside search and notifications. Click it again to close the assistant. If your college holds a certificate with a medal, the **p.** wears the medal's color.

On a policy, in the compliance workspace, and on Ethos configuration, the assistant's **Chat** keeps your conversation, so it is there when you come back. **Your other conversations** at the top of Chat lists your five newest conversations on other pages. Click one to open that page with its assistant.

## Turn an assistant on or off

Only an institutional admin can switch an assistant. Click its switch in the **On** column. Each switch changes only its own assistant.

Other roles read the list and see **Assistants are turned on or off by your college's admin.**

When an assistant is off, its page works without it. Asking it a question returns a line such as **Your college has turned off the reports assistant.**
