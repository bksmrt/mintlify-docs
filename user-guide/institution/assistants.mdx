**Settings** > **Assistants** lists what runs on its own for your college, and what helps on a page when someone asks. An assistant only proposes: nothing it suggests counts until a person confirms it. Every run is kept on the record's History.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, click **Settings**, then **Assistants**. Institutional admins and compliance officers can open Settings.

## What you see

Each row shows:

- **Assistant**: its name and what it does
- **On**: its switch, or **Always on**
- **Last ran**: when it last ran
- **Waiting**: what waits on a person, or **Nothing waits on a person**
- **Decided in**: where its suggestions are decided, often as a link

The list depends on your college's modules.

| Assistant | What it does | Where you decide |
|---|---|---|
| **Mapping assistant** | Reads your policies against an agency's requirements and suggests an owner for each, with the sentence it rests on. The same switch covers **Suggest standards** on a course. | **Compliance Mapping** in Certification Studio |
| **Continuous monitoring** | Reopens evidence that aged past its shelf life or whose standard moved. Runs without a model, and is always on. | Each project's **Evidence** tab |
| **Ethos assistant** | Answers questions about your Ethos settings and who may ask for an exception, and proposes changes for you to set. | **Ethos configuration** |
| **Outcome suggester** | Suggests learning outcomes for a course, for a person to accept. Academic Operations only. | On a course page |
| **Catalog copy editor** | Reads a course's catalog copy against your style and suggests fixes. Academic Operations only. | On a course page |

## Turn an assistant on or off

Only an institutional admin can switch an assistant. Click its switch in the **On** column. Each switch changes only its own assistant.

Other roles read the list and see **Assistants are turned on or off by your college's admin.**
