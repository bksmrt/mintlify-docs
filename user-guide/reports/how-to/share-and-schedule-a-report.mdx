Save a report once, then share it or have it sent on a regular schedule, so people get the latest numbers without asking.

## Before you start

- Open [**Report Studio**](/user-guide/reports/report-studio).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    Ask for it in **What would you like to know?**, or open a standard report such as **Open work**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    At the foot of the report, click **Save this report…**. It appears under **Saved reports**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    From its row, choose **Share…**. Add people and pick **Can view** or **Can comment**. Use **Manage access** to change access later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it">
    From its row, choose **Schedule…**. Pick a **Cadence** (**Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**) and a **Format**. Proof shows **Schedule created**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- People you shared with see the report under **Shared with you**.
- The schedule appears in the **Schedule** column. To keep a copy as it stands now, click **Save snapshot**. To download it, choose **CSV**, **Excel**, or **PDF**.
