Save a report once, then share it or have it sent on a regular schedule, so people get the latest numbers without asking.

## Before you start

- Open [**Report Studio**](/user-guide/reports/report-studio).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    Ask for it in the chat, or open a standard report such as **Open work** from the **Reports** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    At the foot of the report, click **Save this report…**. It appears under **Yours** in the **Reports** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    From the row's **⋯** menu, choose **Share…**. Add people and pick **Can view** or **Can comment**. Use **Manage access** to change access later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it">
    From the row's **⋯** menu, choose **Schedule…**. Under **Send**, pick how often (**Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly, on the 1st**) and the hour. Under **As**, tick **PDF**, **Excel**, or **CSV**. Under **To**, choose **Everyone it’s shared with, and you** or **Choose people…**. To stop the schedule on a date, set **Until**. Click **Schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- People you shared with see the report under **Shared with you**.
- The schedule appears on the report's row. To keep a copy as it stands now, choose **Save snapshot** from the row's **⋯** menu. To download it, choose **Download latest · CSV**, **Download latest · Excel**, or **Download latest · PDF**.
