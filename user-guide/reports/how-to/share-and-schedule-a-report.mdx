Save a report once, then share it or have it sent on a regular schedule, so people get the latest numbers without asking.

## Before you start

- Open [**Report Studio**](/user-guide/reports/report-studio).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    Ask for it in the chat, or open a delivered report such as **Open work** from the **Reports** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    At the foot of the report, click **Save this report…**. It appears under **Yours** in the **Reports** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it inside the college">
    From the row's **⋯** menu, choose **Share…**. In **Add people, a role, a department or a committee**, choose who gets it, and pick **Can view** or **Can comment**. Add a message if you like, then click **Send**. Each person is told, with a link that opens the report. Use **Manage access** to change or remove access later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it outside the college (optional)">
    Under **People outside the college**, choose how long the link lasts (**7 days**, **30 days**, or **90 days**) and click **Make a link**. The link opens a view-only snapshot of the report as it stands today. Click **Copy link** to copy it, and **End link** to stop it early.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it">
    From the row's **⋯** menu, choose **Schedule…**. Under **Send**, pick how often (**Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly, on the 1st**, or **At the month's close**) and the hour. Under **As**, tick **PDF**, **Excel**, or **CSV**. Under **To**, choose **Everyone it’s shared with, and you** or **Choose people…**. If you send a PDF, the **Cover** row sets whether each file carries its **Cover page** and **Illustration**. To stop the schedule on a date, set **Until**. Click **Schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- People you shared with see the report under **Shared with you**. Reports you have shared show a shared icon.
- Anyone who can comment finds the report's discussion drawer at the foot of the report page.
- The schedule appears on the report's row. To change it, choose **Schedule…** again. To stop it, choose **End schedule**.
- To keep a copy as it stands now, choose **Save snapshot** from the row's **⋯** menu. To download it, choose **Download latest · CSV**, **Download latest · Excel**, or **Download latest · PDF**.
- When you export a report's PDF from its page, Proof shows a preview first, where you can leave out the cover. See [The PDF and its cover](/user-guide/reports/delivered-reports#the-pdf-and-its-cover).

<Note>
Some reports stay inside the college and offer no outside link, for example **History** and **Open work**, because they hold people's names and work.
</Note>
