When you need to know who changed something, for an accreditor, an auditor, or your own review, look in History or the Audit Trail.

## Before you start

- To search the whole college, your role must be able to read the college's history.
- Anyone can read a record's own history on its page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from the record, if you know it">
    Open the record, for example a course, and go to its **History** tab. It lists every change to that record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or search the whole college">
    In the sidebar, click [**Audit Trail**](/user-guide/reports/audit-trail). In **Search what happened, a record or a person…**, type the record's name or the person's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the entry">
    Each entry shows **What happened**, the **Record**, who did it, and when.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep a copy">
    For a report you can hand over, open **History** in [**Report Studio**](/user-guide/reports/report-studio), save it, and download it as **PDF** or **Excel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

The Audit Trail is read-only. Nothing you do here changes the record.
