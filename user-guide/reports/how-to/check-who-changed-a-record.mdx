When you need to know who changed something, for an accreditor, an auditor, or your own review, look in History or the Audit Trail.

## Before you start

- To search the whole college, your role must be able to read the college's history.
- Anyone who can open a record can read its own history on its page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from the record, if you know it">
    Open the record, for example a course, and go to its **History** tab. It lists every change to that record. To include what was said in the record's discussion, turn on the **Discussion** switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or search the whole college">
    In the sidebar, click [**Audit Trail**](/user-guide/reports/audit-trail). In **Search what happened, a record or a person…**, type the record's name or the person's name. Use **Filters** to narrow by date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the entry">
    Each entry shows the **Change**, who made it (**By**), and **When**. Click it to read the details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep a copy">
    Click **Export CSV**, **Excel**, or **PDF** above the list. For a report you can save and share, open **History** in [**Report Studio**](/user-guide/reports/report-studio).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

History and the Audit Trail are read-only. Nothing you do here changes the record.
