When no delivered report holds the list you need, describe it in Report Studio. Proof picks the records, columns, and filters, and opens the report beside the chat for you to refine.

## Before you start

- Open [**Report Studio**](/user-guide/reports/report-studio).
- A custom report can list gaps, requirements, evidence, tasks, projects, standards, and other records your college keeps. It shows only the records you may read.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the list">
    Type what you want, for example "every open gap across the college with its standard and days open", and press Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on a Mac). Proof opens the report beside the chat. If your words could mean more than one thing, Proof runs the likeliest reading and lists the others under **Did you mean**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine it in words or with the pills">
    Type a change in the chat, for example "only the critical ones". Or use the pills above the table:

    - **+ Column** adds a column. Click a column's **×** to remove it.
    - **+ Filter** adds a filter. Pick a field, choose what to keep, and click **Add filter**.
    - To move a column, drag its header or its pill, or select it and press Alt+Left or Alt+Right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Undo a change">
    Click **↶ Undo** to step back, and **Redo ↷** to step forward again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Click the report's title and type a new name. Press Enter to keep it, or Escape to cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or export it">
    At the report's foot, click **Save this report…** to keep it under **Yours**. You can also download it as **CSV**, **Excel**, or **PDF**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If Proof does not store a column you ask for

Proof tells you it does not store that field and offers the nearest fields or reports. Institutional admins also see **+ Add "…" as a field the college fills in**. Pick its **Kind**, such as **Number**, **Yes or no**, or a list of values, and click **Add the field**. Other roles see who to ask.

Once added, the field appears on the matching records' pages, where your college fills it in, and as a column in custom reports. Institutional admins can rename, archive, or delete these fields in **Settings** > **Fields**.

## What happens next

- A saved custom report reads your records again each time you open it.
- Share or schedule it like any other report. See [Save, share, and schedule a report](/user-guide/reports/how-to/share-and-schedule-a-report).
