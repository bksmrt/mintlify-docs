Delivered reports are ready-made reports over your college's live records. Each one has its own page with exports as **CSV**, **Excel**, and **PDF**, and each one can be saved, shared, and scheduled.

## How to get here

- In [**Report Studio**](/user-guide/reports/report-studio), open the **Reports** group in the left panel, or click **Reports ▾** beside the chat input.
- Or ask a question the report answers, for example "which exceptions expire this month?". Proof answers in the chat and links to the report.

You see only the reports your role can read. Some reports read only your department's records if that is what your role covers.

## Standing

| Report | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Obligations standing** | Where each requirement stands (met, open, awaits, not yet read), with the sentence that answers it and who last moved it. Read for one project or across the college. |
| **KPIs** | Your college's key measures from the baseline to today, with each snapshot and whether the target is met. |
| **Exceptions register** | Every compliance exception in force, expiring, or expired, plus those granted in the last 90 days: the rule, the department, the change allowed, who decided it, and when it expires. |
| **Compliance calendar** | Every dated obligation from a year overdue to the next 90 days: compliance items, policy reviews, deadlines, unacknowledged regulatory changes, expiring exceptions, and evidence going stale, each with its owner. Choose 30, 90, 180, or 365 days ahead. |
| **Strategic plan alignment** | Each objective of your [strategic plan](/user-guide/institution/strategic-plan) with the projects and program reviews that serve it. An objective with an open high or critical gap is marked at risk. Work that names no objective is listed last. |

## What changed

| Report | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Change summary** | What changed on a project, or across the college, in a period: sections and their versions, readings that moved, gaps opened and closed, and evidence added. |
| **History** | Who did what, across the college or on one record, by period and kind. |
| **Policies as of a date** | Every policy's words as they stood on one day. Drafts and policies archived since are listed apart. |
| **Regulatory change response** | How the college answered each regulatory change in the last 12 months: what still waits for acknowledgement, who acknowledged it and when, how it was resolved, and the median days to acknowledge. |

## Work and decisions

| Report | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Open work** | Every task, hand-off, ask, sign-off, and exception still open, overdue first, with its owner and due date. Read for a project, the college, or one person. |
| **Approval cycle time** | How long each approval took in the last 90 days, step by step against each step's clock, and what still waits. A send-back starts a new round. |

## Faculty and curriculum

These reports belong to the Academic Operations module.

| Report | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Faculty qualifications roster** | Who teaches each section of a term and on what basis: degrees, the qualification basis on record, and who determined it. A section with nothing on record reads as not yet determined, with Proof's suggestion marked. |
| **Credit-hour compliance** | Each section's scheduled minutes against the floor its policy sets. Sections short of the floor come first, with the reason. |
| **Assessment and closing the loop** | Each program outcome's result for a cycle, the action recorded when it fell short, and the later cycle that verified it. |

<Note>
If your college's plan does not include the module a report belongs to, the report opens as a preview. You see **Academic Operations isn't included in your plan**, a sample of up to three of your own rows (or an example college's, labelled as such), and **View plans & pricing**.
</Note>

## Reports opened from a record

- **Phase story**: what one phase of a project did, said, and decided: each role's statement, the task log's entries, and the open gaps with their cause, effect, and rating. On a project's **Tasks** tab, click **Phase story →** on the phase.
- A project's **History** tab opens the **Change summary** for that project and period.

## On every report page

- **Export**: download the report as **CSV**, **Excel**, or **PDF**.
- **Save snapshot**: keep the report as it stands now.
- **Save this report…**: keep the report with its settings so it appears under **Yours** in Report Studio.
