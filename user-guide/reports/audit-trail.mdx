The Audit Trail lists every change recorded against the institution's record: who acted, upon what, and when. It is read-only, with the newest entries first.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, click **Audit Trail**. You see it only if your role can read the college's history. If you can't, Proof explains why. You can still see a record's own history on that record's page, and your own activity.

## What you see

Each entry shows **What happened**, the **Record** it affected, who made it, and when.

<Note>
Some entries hold a student's or a colleague's personal details. These are left out for readers who are not allowed to see them, and Proof tells you how many were left out.
</Note>

## What you can do here

- **Search**: use **Search what happened, a record or a person…**.
- **Show support activity**: turn on **Support** to see capability grants and revokes by your institutional admins, plus any direct intervention by bksmrt staff. Each entry includes the actor and the reason recorded at the time.
- **Clear**: reset the search and filters.
