The Audit Trail lists every change recorded against the institution's record: who acted, upon what, and when. It is read-only, with the newest entries first.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, click **Audit Trail**. You see it only if your role can read the college's history. If you can't, Proof explains why. You can still read a record's own **History** tab on that record's page, and your own activity.

## What you see

Each entry shows the **Change**, who made it (**By**), and **When**. Click an entry to read its details, such as the words that changed or the reason recorded with it.

<Note>
Some entries hold a student's or a colleague's personal details. These are left out for readers who are not allowed to see them, and Proof tells you how many were left out.
</Note>

## What you can do here

- **Search**: use **Search what happened, a record or a person…**.
- **Filter**: open **Filters** to narrow the list by **Record**, **What happened**, **By** (the person who acted), or a **From** and **To** date.
- **Show support activity**: in **Filters**, choose **Support interventions only** to see capability grants and revokes by your institutional admins, plus any direct intervention by bksmrt staff. Each entry includes the actor and the reason recorded at the time.
- **Export**: click **Export CSV**, **Excel**, or **PDF** to download what the list shows.
- **Clear filters**: reset the search and filters.

## A record's own History

Most records, such as a course, program, policy, or project, have a **History** tab with the same list for that record alone.

- If the record has a discussion, turn on the **Discussion** switch beside **Filters** to add its comments to the list. The count beside the switch is the number of comments. Click **Open in the discussion** on a comment to jump to it in the record's discussion drawer.
- A moderator of the discussion sees **Restore** on a deleted or archived comment and can bring it back.
