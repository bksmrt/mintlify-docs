Report Studio is a chat for your college's reports. Ask a question in plain words, open one of the delivered reports, or describe the list you want and Proof builds it for you.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, click **Report Studio**. It appears in the sidebar for institutional admins and compliance officers.

## What you see

Report Studio has two parts: the **Reports** panel on the left and the chat, which fills the rest of the page.

### Ask

When the chat is empty, it shows **What would you like to know?** with a few suggested questions and a **Custom report** card. Type a question, for example "what changed on the BSN self-study in October?". To ask it, press Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on a Mac) or click the send button. Enter starts a new line. Click **Start fresh** to begin a new conversation.

While Proof answers, the send button becomes **Stop**. A question you type meanwhile waits its turn and is asked next. If you stop, the question fades and reads **Not answered**.

To ask about one record, type `/link` and then a few letters of a policy, document, standard, project, gap, or task. Choose it from **Sources you may read** and press Enter. Proof adds it to your question as a source. Answers can also offer **Ask** buttons that start a new question about a record they mention.

Each answer leads with a short summary. Numbered markers in the summary point at the rows they rest on. The answer also offers follow-up questions and a link to open the full report.

### Reports panel

The panel lists every report you can open, with **Search reports…** and filters for **Kind**, **Scope**, **Shared**, and **Scheduled**. Reports are grouped, and you can fold each group:

- **Pinned**: reports you pinned
- **Yours**: reports you saved
- **Shared with you**: reports colleagues have shared with you
- **Reports**: the [delivered reports](/user-guide/reports/delivered-reports), banded by what they answer: **Standing**, **What changed**, **Work and decisions**, and **Faculty and curriculum**. Reports from the older report builder are listed last, under **From the older builder**.

While a report is open beside the chat, the panel folds to a narrow strip of your pinned reports. Click **Open the reports** to expand it. On a narrow screen, open the panel from the **Reports ▾** button at the left of the chat input.

## What you can do here

- **Open a delivered report**: click it in the panel, or ask a question it answers. See [Delivered reports](/user-guide/reports/delivered-reports).
- **Build a custom report**: describe the records you want, for example "every open gap across the college with its standard and days open". Proof opens the report beside the chat. See [Build a custom report](/user-guide/reports/how-to/build-a-custom-report).
- **Save a report**: open it and click **Save this report…** at its foot. It appears under **Yours**.
- **Work with a report**: hover a row or click its **⋯** menu, then choose **Open**, **Send to chat**, **Pin** or **Unpin**, **Save snapshot**, **Download latest · CSV**, **Download latest · Excel**, **Download latest · PDF**, **Share…**, **Schedule…**, **Rename**, or **Archive**. Some reports offer only some of these.
- **Ask about a report**: choose **Send to chat**, or drag the row into the chat.
- **Edit a report you opened elsewhere**: on a report's own page, click **Edit in Report Studio →**. Proof opens the report in the chat, where you can refine it in words.
- **Find a policy or document by topic**: ask for it without its name, for example "any documents about clinical hours?". Proof lists the policies and documents you may read that fit under **Records it found**, each with the passage that matched. Click a title to open it.
- **Open a record from a report**: click a linked name in a report, such as a project, person, gap, or task, to open it.

<Note>
Proof reads every report by your own permissions. If you ask for something across the college and your role covers only your department, Proof answers for your department.
</Note>
