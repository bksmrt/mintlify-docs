Report Studio builds configurable, versioned reports from your live compliance data. Ask a question in plain words, or open one of the standard reports.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, click **Report Studio**.

## What you see

Report Studio has two parts: the **Reports** panel on the left and the chat, which fills the rest of the page.

### Ask

When the chat is empty, it shows **What would you like to know?** Type a question, for example "what changed on the BSN self-study in October?". To ask it, press Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on a Mac) or click the send button. Enter starts a new line. Click **Start fresh** to begin a new conversation.

To ask about one record, type `/link` and then a few letters of a policy, document, standard, project, gap, or task. Choose it from **Sources you may read** and press enter. Proof adds it to your question as a source. Answers can also offer **Ask** buttons that start a new question about a record they mention.

### Reports panel

The panel lists every report you can open, with **Search reports…** and filters for **Kind**, **Scope**, **Shared**, and **Scheduled**. Reports are grouped, and you can fold each group:

- **Pinned**: reports you pinned
- **Yours**: reports you saved
- **Shared with you**: reports colleagues have shared with you
- **Reports**: the standard reports, grouped by what they answer: **Standing** (**Obligations standing**, **KPIs**), **What changed** (**Change summary**, **History**), and **Work and decisions** (**Open work**)

While a report is open beside the chat, the panel folds to a narrow strip of your pinned reports. Click **Open the reports** to expand it. On a narrow screen, open the panel from the **Reports ▾** button at the left of the chat input.

## What you can do here

- **Build a custom report**: ask for the records you want, for example "every open gap across the college with its standard and days open". Proof opens the report beside the chat. Refine it by typing in the chat, or change its **Columns** and click **Add filter**. To move a column, drag its header or its pill, or select it and press Alt+Left or Alt+Right.
- **Save a report**: open it and click **Save this report…** at its foot. It appears under **Yours**.
- **Work with a report**: hover a row or click its **⋯** menu, then choose **Open**, **Send to chat**, **Pin** or **Unpin**, **Save snapshot**, **Download latest · CSV**, **Download latest · Excel**, **Download latest · PDF**, **Share…**, **Schedule…**, **Rename**, or **Archive**. Some reports offer only some of these.
- **Edit a report you opened elsewhere**: on a report's own page, click **Edit in Report Studio →**. Proof opens the report in the Report Studio chat, where you can refine it in words.
- **Open a record from a report**: click a linked name in a report, such as a project, person, gap, or task, to open it.
- **Ask about a report**: choose **Send to chat**, or drag the row into the chat.
- **Add a field the college fills in**: if you ask for a column Proof does not store, institutional admins see **+ Add "…" as a field the college fills in**. Pick its **Kind** and click **Add the field**. Institutional admins can also manage these fields in **Settings** > **Fields**.
