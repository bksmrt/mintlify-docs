Report Studio builds configurable, versioned reports from your live compliance data. Ask a question in plain words, or open one of the standard reports.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, click **Report Studio**.

## What you see

### Ask

At the top is a box: **What would you like to know?** Type a question, for example "what changed on the BSN self-study in October?", and press enter. Click **Start fresh** to begin a new conversation.

### Saved reports

Your saved reports, each with its **Latest snapshot** and **Schedule**. Until you save one, this list says: "Nothing saved yet. Open a report and press **Save this report…** at its foot."

### Shared with you

Reports colleagues have shared with you.

### Standard reports

- **History**: what changed across the college's record
- **Open work**: what is still in progress and with whom
- **Obligations standing**: where each compliance obligation stands
- **Change summary**: a summary of recent changes
- **Pilot KPIs**: key measures for the pilot

## What you can do here

- **Save a report**: open it and click **Save this report…** at its foot.
- **Work with a saved report**: from its row, click **Open live**, download it as **CSV**, **Excel**, or **PDF**, or choose **Share…**, **Schedule…**, **Rename**, or **Archive**.
- **Create a custom report**: click **New custom report** and give it a **Report name**.
