Proof is a multi-tenant compliance and accreditation platform for higher education institutions and the consulting firms that support them. It helps teams manage accreditation projects, track regulatory standards, collect evidence, and prepare for review. This site documents the integration surface: how to connect your identity provider, sync curriculum and faculty data from your student information system, import documents from cloud storage, and consume webhook events.

## Before you start

You configure integrations inside the Proof web app, not through a public API or command-line tool. Most of it lives in **Settings**, which shows the **Integrations** and **Webhooks** tabs to members of an institution.

Who can do what:

| Integration | Who manages it |
| --- | --- |
| **Webhooks** (**Settings › Webhooks**) | `institutional_admin` |
| **SIS feed** (**Settings › Integrations**, **Student records**) | `institutional_admin` manages it. A `compliance_officer` can read it, and manage it once an admin grants SIS management on the **Users** page. |
| **Cloud storage** (**Settings › Integrations**, **Storage**, and **Import from cloud** in a project's evidence tab) | Any member can connect an account and import from the evidence tab. Only an `institutional_admin`, or a firm consultant on an engagement at the institution, can disconnect one. |
| **Single sign-on** | Set up with your Proof operator, who manages the connection from the operator console. See [SSO overview](/sso/overview). |

If you do not see the tab you need, ask an existing institutional administrator at your organization to assign you the `institutional_admin` role.
