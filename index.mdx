Proof is a multi-tenant compliance and accreditation platform for higher education institutions and the consulting firms that support them. It helps teams manage accreditation projects, track regulatory standards, collect evidence, and prepare for review. This site documents the integration surface: how to connect your identity provider, sync curriculum and faculty data from your student information system, import documents from cloud storage, and consume webhook events.

## Before you start

Integration configuration is done inside the Proof web app, not through a public API or command line tool. To manage integrations, you must sign in as a user with administrative authority for your institution.

The routes for integrations, SSO, and webhooks enforce the following admin roles:

- **SSO and webhooks**: available to `institutional_admin` users
- **SIS integrations and cloud storage**: available to `institutional_admin` users, and some actions (such as importing into an engagement) also allow firm consultants who have been granted access to that specific engagement
- **Disconnecting a storage account**: restricted to `institutional_admin`

If you do not see the Integrations, SSO, or Webhooks sections in the admin sidebar, contact an existing institutional administrator at your organization to assign you the `institutional_admin` role.
