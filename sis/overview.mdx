Proof connects to your institution's Student Information System (SIS) to pull curriculum and faculty data into the accreditation workflow. Proof is not an SIS: it never stores students, grades, enrollments, or registration records. The integration reads a mirror of courses, departments, programs, terms, and faculty, while your SIS remains the system of record.

## What syncs and what does not

Proof pulls select data sets from an SIS and stamps each record with provenance so you always know which rows came from an external source. A synced record carries a tag naming its feed, and a record the feed no longer sends reads **Archived from SIS**.

| Direction | Data that syncs | Data that does not sync |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Into Proof | Courses, departments, academic programs, terms, faculty | Students, grades, enrollments, registration, sections, schedules |
| Back to SIS | Nothing from the CSV feed. See [Writeback](/sis/writeback). | |

## Supported feeds

A college can connect one kind of feed today: the **SFTP / CSV Drop**, where your registrar's system writes CSV files to an SFTP folder and Proof reads them. It works with any SIS that can export CSV.

| Vendor | Status | Can a college connect it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SFTP / CSV Drop | Active | Yes, in **Settings › Integrations** |
| Ellucian Banner | Beta | Not yet |
| Ellucian Colleague | Beta | Not yet |
| Workday Student | Beta | Not yet |
| Oracle PeopleSoft Campus Solutions | Coming soon | No |
| Jenzabar One | Coming soon | No |

The beta vendors have API connectors in Proof that have not been run end to end against a vendor sandbox, so they are not offered to colleges. See [Vendor connectors](/sis/vendor-connectors).

## Who can configure an SIS feed

- **Institutional admins** can connect, change, pause, sync, and disconnect the feed.
- **Compliance officers** can see the feed and its runs. An institutional admin can grant a compliance officer SIS management by name on the **Users** page; with the grant, they can manage the feed too. Without it, the section says "An admin can grant you SIS management on the Users page."
- **Other roles** (faculty, department heads, auditors, students) do not see the **Student records** section.

Consulting firms engaged with your institution see a read-only summary of its feeds from the engagement page. They cannot create or change them. Proof operators can read your feed but cannot change it.

## Connection lifecycle

In the Proof app, the SIS feed lives in **Settings › Integrations**, in the **Student records** section. A college has one primary feed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the folder">
    Click **Connect the registrar’s drop**. Under **Where the files are**, enter the **Server**, **Port**, **Folder**, and **Signs in as** fields, plus a **Password** or **Or a private key**. Click **Test the folder**. Proof reads nothing until this test passes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what comes in">
    The test lists the files it found. Tick the ones to bring in. Faculty is off until you turn it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the columns">
    Click **Next: columns**. Proof matches each field to a column in its file by name and shows a preview under each file. Check that the preview reads the way your catalog should.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it runs">
    Click **Next: when it runs**. Under **Runs**, pick **Nightly, around 2:00**, **Weekly, around 2:00**, or **Only when someone presses Sync now**. Give the feed a **Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and preview">
    Click **Save and preview**. The first run is a **Preview**: it shows what would be filed and what would wait on you. Nothing is written until you press **Sync now** or the first scheduled run happens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review runs">
    The **Runs** list shows the last 10 runs. Open a run to see what was filed and what waits on you. Use **Preview**, **Sync now**, and **Pause** or **Resume** at the top of the section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the feed cannot sign in to the folder, its stamp reads **Failing** and the section offers **Update the SFTP password**. Once the new password signs in, the feed is live again. **Disconnect** removes the feed.
